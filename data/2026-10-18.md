### [1] [Four-dimensional QCD equation of state with a critical surface from contours of constant entropy density](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20806)
*Tristan Gyure,Hitansh Shah,Lorenzo Formaggio,Francesco Di Clemente,Mauricio Hippert,Claudia Ratti,Volodymyr Vovchenko*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文提出首个基于格点QCD的四维物态方程（T, μ_B, μ_Q, μ_S），通过球坐标参数化实现含临界曲面的外推，重构压强并给出临界点位置及重离子碰撞下的热力学性质。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有格点QCD物态方程多为低化学势交叉区的泰勒展开外推，无法描述一阶相变与临界点；而重离子碰撞、中子星及早期宇宙等物理场景需要覆盖多种守恒荷（重子数、电荷、奇异数）的完整四维物态方程，并纳入临界曲面与一阶相变。

Method: 将恒定熵密度等值线的展开推广到QCD的多个守恒荷，允许一阶相变的存在；用球坐标 (μ,θ,φ) 参数化化学势空间，把问题化为固定角度下的 (T,μ) 一维外推，从而张成整个四维空间；通过对熵密度积分重构压强，再导出守恒荷密度、磁化率及声速。

Result: 在低化学势交叉区与其它格点QCD构造成果定量吻合；在更高化学势预言一阶相变的出现。在重离子碰撞条件 n_S=0, n_Q=0.4n_B 下，临界点位于 (T_c, μ_{B,c}, μ_{Q,c}, μ_{S,c})=(115±7, 646±72, -12±3, 117±16) MeV。在化学冻出线 √s_NN ≲ 7.7 GeV 处发现重子数磁化率随化学势呈强烈的非单调依赖。

Conclusion: 成功构建了首套带临界曲面的四维格点QCD物态方程，验证了方法的可靠性并给出临界点定量预言；该方法可进一步推广至中子星电荷中性物质与早期宇宙大同位旋密度等场景。

Abstract: We present a new lattice-QCD-based equation of state in the four-dimensional space of $(T,μ_B,μ_Q,μ_S)$ which features a critical surface. This is obtained by generalizing the expansion of constant entropy density contours across the multiple conserved charges of QCD. Since this expansion permits the presence of a first-order phase transition, this is the first lattice QCD based equation of state in four dimensions with a critical surface. We parametrize the chemical potential space using spherical coordinates $(μ,θ,\varphi)$, reducing the problem to a one-dimensional extrapolation in $(T,μ)$ at fixed angles that, together, span the full 4D space. By integrating the extrapolated entropy density, we reconstruct the pressure, from which we obtain the conserved charge densities, their susceptibilities and the speed of sound. Our results agree quantitatively with other lattice QCD constructions at low chemical potentials in the crossover region, and predict the emergence of a first-order phase transition at higher chemical potentials. We study equation of state properties in detail under heavy-ion collision (HIC) conditions for the strangeness and electric charge densities ($n_S=0,~n_Q=0.4n_B$), where we find the CP location at $(T_c,μ_{B,c},μ_{Q,c},μ_{S,c})=(115\pm7,646\pm72,-12\pm3,117\pm16)$ MeV. In particular, we find a strong non-monotonic dependence of baryon number susceptibilities along the chemical freeze-out line at $\sqrt{s_{NN}} \lesssim 7.7$ GeV. Finally, we outline further applications of the 4D equation of state, including charge-neutral matter relevant to neutron stars and large isospin density relevant to cosmic trajectories in the early Universe.

### [2] [Effective shear viscosity from the friction term in relativistic multi-fluid hydrodynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20872)
*Christian Spieles,Marcus Bleicher*

Main category: nucl-th

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 重离子碰撞中系统的非平衡动力学难以用理想流体描述，需要引入粘滞效应；但单流体粘滞流体力学在处理强非平衡和两核穿透阶段存在困难。作者希望通过三流体模型中天然的核摩擦机制，为剪切应力提供一个动态、自洽的来源，从而在非平衡框架内定量理解有效剪切粘度。

Method: 采用三流体流体力学描述重离子碰撞，将弹核、靶核和火球视为各自始终处于局域热平衡的理想流体；由于叠加系统的局域能动张量在其Landau静止系中呈现各向异性，可从中得到非零剪切应力密度和Landau速度场梯度，进而依据Navier-Stokes关系反推"有效剪切粘度"η_eff。文中给出η_eff及生成熵密度随时间的演化，覆盖束流能量范围1.23 A GeV ≤ E_lab ≤ 29.7 A GeV。

Result: 在不同束流能量（1.23–29.7 A GeV）下，得到了η_eff和熵密度s随时间演化的结果，并据此确定了无量纲比值η_eff/s。该比值是在非平衡摩擦模型中动态、自洽地确定的，而非外部预设参数。

Conclusion: 三流体方法中的核摩擦能够作为剪切应力的有效来源，并可动态自洽地给出有效剪切粘度及η_eff/s；结果可与流体力学中的其他计算方法以及微观输运模型给出的估计进行对照。

Abstract: Within a three-fluid description of heavy-ion collisions, we investigate how nuclear friction can be used to model shear stress. In this multi-fluid approach, the individual fluids (projectile, target and fireball) are each treated as locally equilibrated ideal fluids at all times, while the local energy-momentum tensor of the superposed system exhibits characteristic anisotropies in its Landau rest frame. From the corresponding non-vanishing shear stress densities and the gradients of the Landau velocity field, we infer an ``effective shear viscosity'' according to the Navier-Stokes relation. We present the time evolution of the resulting values of $η_{\rm eff}$ and the generated entropy density in nuclear collisions at different beam energies ($1.23\,A\text{GeV} \le E_{\mathrm{lab}} \le 29.7\,A\text{GeV}$). The resulting dimensionless ratio $η_{\rm eff}/s$ is determined dynamically and self-consistently within the non-equilibrium friction model. We discuss the relation to previous calculations using alternative methods in hydrodynamics and to estimates based on microscopic transport models.

### [3] [Algebraic coefficients of fractional parentage for three particles in the oscillator Jacobi basis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21385)
*A. Stepšys,S. Mickevičius,D. Germanas*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文给出三个全同粒子在平移不变谐振子 Jacobi 基中分数母系数（CFP）的闭式构造，避免了对置换算符进行数值对角化，只需单个酉变换 W 与单列 Ket 即可得到 CFP，精度极高且可快速验证。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统计算三全同粒子 CFP 需要在每个能量-角动量块中数值对角化置换算符，成本高且引入数值误差。作者希望消除这一对角化步骤，用闭式解析构造直接得到 CFP。

Method: 用固定复化（complexification）与质量参数 d=1 的 HO 括号（bracket）构成单一酉变换 W=H¹Φ，将类算符 P₁₃+P₂₃ 化装（dressing）为标签交换乘以整数；每个 CFP 是单列 Ket |K_{i,s}⟩=2Q^s W†|i⟩（2 倍缩放、带符号的 d=1 括号列，并限制在标签的一个 e₂ 宇称分支），其范数 1+s⟨i|J|i⟩ 为单个矩阵元；同一 W 还把标签宇称映射为交换对称性 W Q^s W†=R^s。特征值为精确整数 ±2、±1，标记 S(3) 对称类。该方法对 E、L 与对称类均一致，把 CFP 专属工作量从 O(n³) 降到 O(n²)。

Result: 在 SU(3) 基括号引擎上实现并检查了 E≤50 的全部 1301 个块（912366 个 CFP，最大块 2907×2907）：S(3) 类指派处处精确；残留数值误差完全来自括号引擎，E≤35 时低于 10⁻⁸，更高能级通过四精度构建恢复到 ≤10⁻²²。与独立生成的三核子程序反对称化数据（含 ³He 基态能量）交叉验证一致。

Conclusion: 三全同粒子 CFP 的数值对角化完全可被一个闭式酉变换构造替代，显著降低计算复杂度并大幅提高数值精度，且已在大的参数范围内及三核子物理应用中验证。

Abstract: We present a closed-form construction of the orbital coefficients of fractional parentage (CFP) of three identical particles in the translationally invariant harmonic oscillator (HO) Jacobi basis. These are usually obtained by numerically diagonalizing a permutation operator in every energy-angular-momentum block. We show this diagonalization is unnecessary. Dressing the class operator $P_{13} + P_{23}$ with the single unitary $W = H^1 Φ$ -- a fixed complexification followed by the HO bracket with mass parameter $d=1$ -- turns it into the label swap times an integer. Every CFP is then the single ket $|K_{i,s}\rangle = 2Q^s W^\dagger|i\rangle$ -- a 2-scaled, sign-decorated column of the $d=1$ bracket restricted to one $e_2$-parity of the labels -- whose norm, $1 + s\langle i | J | i \rangle$, is itself a single matrix element. The same $W$ carries label parity into swap symmetry, $W Q^s W^\dagger = R^s$, making the bookkeeping collapse. The eigenvalues are exact integers $\pm 2$ and $\pm 1$ labeling the $S(3)$ symmetry classes. The construction is uniform in $E$, $L$ and symmetry class, reducing CFP-specific work from $O(n^3)$ to $O(n^2)$ in block dimension. An eigensolver remains only inside the bracket engine supplying $H^1$, where all residual numerical error originates. We implement this on an SU(3)-basis bracket engine, checking all 1301 blocks up to $E=50$ (912 366 CFPs, largest block $2907 \times 2907$): the $S(3)$ class assignment is exact everywhere. The residual numerical error (entirely the bracket engine's) stays below $10^{-8}$ for $E \leq 35$, restoring to $\leq 10^{-22}$ via a quadruple-precision build beyond that. We further validate against independently generated antisymmetrization data of a production three-nucleon code, including the $^3$He ground-state energy.
