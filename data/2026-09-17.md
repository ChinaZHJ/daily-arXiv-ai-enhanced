### [1] [Learning Nuclear Structure with AI: Radii and Collectivity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17838)
*Giuliano Giacalone,Sokratis Trifinopoulos,Mike Williams*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文提出 NuCLR（Nuclear Co-Learned Representations）多任务核数据模型，利用共享表征同时学习电荷半径与电四极跃迁强度 B(E2)，并通过留出集成（held-out ensembles）与折外验证（OOF）证明共享表征优于单任务学习，预测精度可与最先进核模型竞争。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 低能核结构信息广泛分散于核素图的大量实验数据中，传统单任务模型难以挖掘跨可观测量与跨核素的潜在组织规律。作者希望构建一个数据驱动的多任务基线，为理论外推和实验设计提供经验依据。

Method: 构建 NuCLR 多任务核数据模型，对电荷半径与 B(E2) 电四极跃迁强度进行联合学习，采用共享表征（co-learned representations）；通过留出集成与折外（OOF）验证评估泛化性能，并与单任务学习及现有核模型对比，同时用误差棒估计核素图各区域的预期预测精度。

Result: 折外验证表明共享表征优于单任务学习：电荷半径 RMS 偏差为 0.0147 fm，B(E2) RMS 偏差为 0.192 e²b²，覆盖数百个核素，精度可与最先进核模型竞争；误差棒标定了核素图上预测精度的区域差异，指出哪些区域的新数据能带来超出已学模式的信息。

Conclusion: NuCLR 可作为一种数据驱动的核结构"勘测者"，推动构建共享、多可观测量、面向核素图的基础模型，为核结构研究与实验设计提供数据驱动的经验基准。

Abstract: Low-energy nuclear structure is encoded in a broad body of experimental information across the chart of nuclides. Learning how this information is organized across observables and nuclei can provide a data-driven empirical baseline for theoretical extrapolations and experimental design. Here, we develop held-out ensembles based on NuCLR (Nuclear Co-Learned Representations), a multi-task model of nuclear data, to study charge radii and electric-quadrupole transition strengths. Out-of-fold (OOF) validation shows that shared representation improves performance over single-task learning, yielding a charge-radius $\mathrm{RMS}$ deviation of $0.0147~{\rm fm}$ and a $\mathrm{B(E2)}$ $\mathrm{RMS}$ deviation of $0.192~e^2{\rm b}^2$ across hundreds of nuclides, competitive with state-of-the-art nuclear models. Our error bars estimate the expected prediction accuracy across the nuclear chart, highlighting regions where new data would encode information beyond the learned patterns. NuCLR thus serves as a data-driven surveyor of nuclear structure and a step toward a shared, multi-observable foundation model of the nuclear chart.

### [2] [From Nuclear Many-Body Correlations to Energy Detector Correlators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18754)
*João Barata,Giuliano Giacalone*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文提出一种将高能核碰撞中初态多体关联与末态探测器关联量系统性匹配的形式体系：以渐进能量流构造探测器关联子，通过小-x QCD将角投影映射到碰撞核内的多极算符关联，从而为入射与出射 QCD 态的长波关联建立可系统性改进的匹配框架。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 相对论核碰撞为研究核基态多体关联提供了实验途径，但初态关联与对撞机上末态多粒子观测量之间的联系尚缺乏可系统性改进的匹配方案。作者希望借助小-x QCD 与能量流关联子，弥合初态核结构与末态测量之间的理论鸿沟。

Method: 作者将渐进能量流表示为早期应力张量的泛函，并在合适模式下展开，在主阶恢复熟悉的线性流体力学关系；随后以小-x QCD 为动机，把探测器关联子的角投影映射到碰撞核内计算的多极算符关联，从而建立系统性的匹配形式体系。

Result: 文章建立了将末态能量流关联子与初态多极算符关联相联系的系统性形式体系，在长波关联层面实现了入射与出射 QCD 态的匹配，并在主阶还原线性流体力学关系。

Conclusion: 以探测器能量流关联子为中介，可以系统性地把高能强子碰撞中的初态核关联与末态多粒子观测连接起来，为后续逐阶改进的匹配计算提供了自然框架。

Abstract: Relativistic nuclear collisions have opened an experimental arena for studying many-body correlations in nuclear ground states. However, the connection between initial-state correlations and final-state multi-particle observables measured at colliders is not yet formulated as a systematically improvable matching problem. We show that detector correlators built from asymptotic energy flows provide a natural framework for realizing such a construction. In particular, we express the asymptotic energy flow as a functional of the early-time stress tensor, and expand it in suitable modes to recover the familiar linear hydrodynamic relations at leading order. Then, motivated by small-$x$ QCD, we map angular projections of detector correlators to multipole-operator correlators computed in the colliding nuclei. We thus establish a systematic formalism for matching long-wavelength correlations between incoming and outgoing QCD states in high-energy hadronic collisions.

### [3] [Alpha-cluster Formation and Decay: Four-Body Correlation and Configuration Mixing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17944)

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文提出一种改进的四集团波函数方法（QWFA），通过引入粒子数投影的BCS（PBCS）方法考虑开壳核中配对引起的组态混合，从而研究α衰变中α团簇的形成与衰变，发现配对增强开壳核的α团簇形成振幅，而闭壳抑制α成团。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: α衰变是探测核结构和核子-核子相互作用的重要手段，但从第一性原理出发的严格微观描述仍具挑战。虽然α+幻数核体系已能通过四体关联和泡利阻塞实现α团簇形成与衰变的微观处理，但将其推广到开壳核时，还需考虑费米面附近价核子间剩余相互作用所导致的组态混合。

Method: 本文改进四集团波函数方法（QWFA），采用粒子数投影的Bardeen-Cooper-Schrieffer（PBCS）方法引入配对引起的组态混合，从而将α团簇的微观描述推广到开壳核体系。

Result: 研究发现，配对增强开壳核中α团簇的形成振幅，而闭壳层则抑制α成团现象。

Conclusion: 通过将配对诱导的组态混合纳入四集团波函数方法，成功将α衰变的微观描述推广至开壳核，并揭示了配对与壳层结构对α团簇形成的影响规律。

Abstract: Alpha-decay provides an important probe of nuclear structure and the underlying nucleon-nucleon interaction, while its rigorous microscopic description from first principles remains challenging. For $α$+magic core systems, a microscopic treatment of $α$-cluster formation and decay has been achieved by considering both the four-body correlation on top of the core and Pauli blocking. Extending this microscopic description to open-shell nuclei should account for the configuration mixing caused by the residual interactions between valence nucleons near the Fermi surface. In this work, we improve the quartetting wave function approach (QWFA) by incorporating the pairing-induced configuration mixing using the particle-number-projected Bardeen-Cooper-Schrieffer (PBCS) method. We find that the pairing enhance the formation amplitudes of the $α$-cluster in open-shell nuclei while the closed shells suppress the $α$-clustering.

### [4] [Sensitivity of the $^{229m}$Th clock transition to the fine-structure constant in a Skyrme-Hartree-Fock-BCS approach](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18696)
*Nikolay Minkov,Adriana Pálffy*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文在自洽 Hartree-Fock + BCS 框架下（采用 Skyrme 能量密度泛函），考虑核形状形变与对关联，理论研究了 $^{229m}$Th 异构体跃迁频率对精细结构常数 α 随时间变化的敏感度。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: $^{229m}$Th 具有极低（可达 keV 以下）的核异构体跃迁频率，使其成为检测精细结构常数 α 随时间变化的理想候选体系。需要从微观核模型出发，自洽地定量预言其跃迁频率对 α 的敏感度 K 及其背后的物理条件。

Method: 采用自洽 Hartree-Fock + Bardeen-Cooper-Schrieffer (HF+BCS) 方法，结合 Skyrme 能量密度泛函，详细计入核形状形变（尤其轴向八极形变）和对关联效应；同时计算 Coulomb 能与异构体能，并通过微调对关联强度获得低异构体能解。

Result: 通过精细调节对关联强度并考虑轴向八极形变，模型能够预言低于 keV 极限的极低异构体能；而在施加反射对称性约束时无法得到该结果。具有八极形变的解中，异构态与基态间的 Coulomb 能差比强制反射对称性情形大一个数量级，最终给出异构体跃迁频率对 α 的敏感度 K∼10³–10⁴。

Conclusion: 该工作首次在微观核模型框架内完全理论性地预言了 $^{229m}$Th 异构体跃迁频率对 α 的敏感度 K∼10³–10⁴，并揭示八极形变与对关联是理解其物理条件的关键。

Abstract: The sensitivity of the $^{229m}$Th isomer transition frequency to the possible temporal variation of the fine-structure constant $α$ is investigated theoretically. We evaluate both the Coulomb as well as the isomer energies in a selfconsistent Hartree-Fock plus Bardeen-Cooper-Schrieffer (BCS) approach with Skyrme energy density functional, taking a detailed account of the nuclear shape deformation and pairing correlations. Our results show that by a fine tuning of the pairing strengths and considering axial octupole deformation, the model can predict a very low isomer energy below the keV limit, which is not accessible in the presence of imposed reflection symmetry. Furthermore, in the model solution with octupole deformation, the difference between the Coulomb energy in the isomeric and ground states is one order of magnitude larger than for the case with imposed reflection symmetry. These results allow for a fully theoretical prediction of the sensitivity of the isomer transition frequency to $α$ in the framework of a microscopic nuclear model, yielding values of $K\sim 10^3-10^4$, and for a deeper understanding of its underlying physical conditions.

### [5] [Constraining the $f_0(980)$ Structure and Formation Dynamics via Anisotropy-Response Scaling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17765)
*Roy A. Lacey*

Main category: nucl-ex

TL;DR: 本文利用物种分辨的各向异性响应标度框架研究相对论核碰撞中 $f_0(980)$ 的结构与形成动力学，发现单一介子型响应标度可闭合实验数据，但无法唯一区分结构；结合响应构造与有效末态响应参数 $ζ_{f_0}$ 可将 $f_0(980)$ 刻画为弱末态敏感的单介子型响应，区别于分子态基准。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: $f_0(980)$ 的内部结构（如是否为 ${\rm K\bar K}$ 分子态）在核碰撞环境下尚未被实验唯一判定，需要新的实验探针来区分其形成动力学与结构。

Method: 采用物种分辨的各向异性响应标度框架，对比单一介子响应构造与对称 ${\rm K\bar K}$ 组份响应构造的标度闭合性；并引入显式分子态 ${\rm K\bar K}$ 计算作为基准，考察其有效末态响应 $ζ_{f_0}$ 的大小。

Result: 高多重数 p+Pb 碰撞（$\sqrt{s_{NN}}=8.16$ TeV）的已发表测量在单一介子响应构造下呈现宽范围标度闭合，且有效末态响应很小（$ζ_{f_0}\simeq0.04$）；对称 ${\rm K\bar K}$ 组份构造的闭合性明显较差；显式分子态 ${\rm K\bar K}$ 计算虽也呈单介子型标度，但其 $ζ_{f_0}\simeq0.16$ 明显更大。

Conclusion: 单一介子标度本身不是唯一的结构判别依据；但结合响应构造与 $ζ_{f_0}$ 约束，实测 $f_0(980)$ 表现为弱末态敏感的单介子型各向异性响应，与分子态基准显著不同。该工作确立了各向异性响应标度作为 $f_0(980)$ 形成动力学的实验探针，并倡导在不同碰撞系统与束流能量下开展系统性检验。

Abstract: A species-resolved anisotropy-response scaling framework is used to investigate the structure and formation dynamics of the $f_0(980)$ in relativistic nuclear collisions. Published measurements in high-multiplicity p+Pb collisions at $\sqrt{s_{NN}}=8.16$~TeV exhibit broad scaling closure under a single-meson response construction, with a small effective final-state response, $ζ_{f_0}\simeq0.04$, whereas a symmetric ${\rm K\bar K}$ constituent-response construction gives substantially poorer closure. An explicitly molecular ${\rm K\bar K}$ calculation also exhibits single-meson-like scaling but gives a substantially larger effective final-state response, $ζ_{f_0}\simeq0.16$. Thus, single-meson scaling alone is not a unique structural discriminator. The combined response construction and $ζ_{f_0}$ constraints characterize the measured $f_0(980)$ by a single-meson-like anisotropy response with weak final-state sensitivity, distinctly different from the molecular benchmark. These results establish anisotropy-response scaling as a new experimental probe of $f_0(980)$ formation dynamics and motivate systematic tests across collision systems and beam energies.

</details>
