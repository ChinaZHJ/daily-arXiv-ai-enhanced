### [1] [Directional correlations in nuclear charge-radius model residuals enable extrapolation with calibrated uncertainties](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25624)
*Debodyuti Kar,Soumya Bagchi,Timo Dickel,Rituparna Kanungo*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 论文识别出核电荷半径残差中显著的各向异性：沿同位素链（固定Z，改变N）残差可跨越多个中子步保持相关，而沿同中子素链（固定N，改变Z）仅一步质子后即几乎完全去相关。基于此提出ARCUS方法，用各向异性核回归校正并校准预测区间，将两种全局模型误差减半，且在独立盲测集上保持近标称的覆盖，同时给出1008个未测核的排序预测。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 发现WS*与HFB-25两种结构迥异的全局核模型存在共同的残差相关模式（各向异性），表明这是共享的模型缺陷而非个别模型的人为误差。因此需要一种能够编码这一方向性残差结构的校正方法，以提升预测精度和不确定性的可靠性。

Method: 提出ARCUS（各向异性残差校准与不确定性缩放），采用各向异性核回归对残差进行校正，并对预测区间做经验校准。方法通过样本外交叉验证（out-of-fold cross-validation）训练，在129个核的时间盲测集上检验外推覆盖能力，并与各向同性核（匹配相同交叉验证覆盖）进行对照。

Result: ARCUS在样本外交叉验证中将WS*和HFB-25的均方根误差均降低约两倍。在129个核的独立时间盲测集上预测区间保持近标称覆盖；而各向同性核在盲测集上显著高估不确定性，说明可靠校准迁移依赖方向性残差结构的编码。在^52Ca等反常结构区域，ARCUS预测区间保持足够宽度以覆盖增大的误差。ARCUS进一步将校准预测扩展到1008个未测核并按不确定性排序。

Conclusion: 编码残差的方向性各向异性结构是实现可靠校准迁移的关键。ARCUS通过各向异性核回归大幅降低了全局模型的预测误差，并在外推情景下提供可靠的不确定性量化，可为未来电荷半径测量提供按不确定性排序的候选目标。

Abstract: We identify a pronounced directional anisotropy in the residuals between measured nuclear charge radii and two structurally distinct global models: the phenomenological Weizsäcker--Skyrme formula (WS*) and the microscopic Hartree--Fock--Bogoliubov model (HFB-25). In both cases, the residuals remain correlated over several neutron steps along isotopic chains but decorrelate almost completely after a single proton step along isotonic chains. This common pattern, reinforced by a strong correlation between the two residual fields ($r\simeq0.73$), points to a shared deficiency of both global descriptions rather than a model-specific artefact. Motivated by this geometry, we introduce ARCUS (Anisotropic Residual Calibration with Uncertainty Scaling), which applies an anisotropic kernel-regression correction with empirically calibrated prediction intervals. In out-of-fold cross-validation, ARCUS reduces the root-mean-square errors of both WS* and HFB-25 by approximately a factor of two. On an independent, temporally blind test set of 129 nuclei, its prediction intervals retain coverage close to nominal under extrapolation. An isotropic kernel matched to the same cross-validation coverage instead substantially overestimates uncertainties on the blind set, showing that reliable calibration transfer depends on encoding the directional residual structure. In regions with anomalous structure, such as around $^{52}$Ca, ARCUS keeps its prediction intervals wide enough to cover the increased errors, rather than yielding overconfident point predictions. We also extend these calibrated predictions to 1008 unmeasured nuclei near known isotopic chains, ranked by uncertainty to support the future charge-radius measurements.

### [2] [Evidence for Quartet Binding of Valence Neutrons in $^8$He](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25883)
*Young-Ho Song,Yuan-Zhuo Ma,Dean Lee*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文提出证据表明 ^8He 的四个价中子构成了有限系统中四中子（quartet）束缚态的类比，其中 Daejeon16 无核壳模型计算显示，^1S_0 配对与 ^3P_2 吸引的协同作用是这种非加和束缚的主导来源。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 多模中子超流理论预言 quartet 是由两个自旋单态 s 波中子对形成的束缚态，但此前缺乏有限系统中该现象的明确对应；需要确定四中子束缚不仅依赖中子-中子吸引强度，也依赖足够强吸引对模式的数量与对称性，并厘清其微观相互作用来源。

Method: 作者以 ^8He 的四个价中子为研究对象，采用 Daejeon16 无核壳模型（no-core shell model）进行分波分析，解析中子-中子相互作用中各分波的贡献；同时利用密度累积量（density cumulants）探测四中子的连通关联，以识别非加和（nonadditive）四体束缚。

Result: 结果表明 Pauli 阻塞限制了同一对结构的重复利用，而额外的对模式可提供额外束缚；分波分析确认协同作用的 ^1S_0 配对与额外的 ^3P_2 吸引是中子-中子相互作用对非加和束缚的主要贡献，密度累积量揭示了连通四中子关联的存在。

Conclusion: ^8He 的四个价中子实现了无限系统中四中子（quartet）束缚的有限系统类比，其非加和束缚由协同的 ^1S_0 配对和 ^3P_2 吸引主导，印证了多模配对模式对四体束缚的重要性。

Abstract: Multimodal neutron superfluidity predicts quartets formed as bound states of two spin-singlet $s$-wave neutron pairs. We present evidence that the four valence neutrons of $^8$He realize the finite-system analogue. Quartet binding depends not only on the strength of neutron-neutron attraction but also on the number and symmetry of sufficiently strong attractive pair modes. Pauli blocking limits reuse of the same pair structure, while additional modes can provide extra binding. A partial-wave analysis of the neutron-neutron interaction in Daejeon16 no-core shell-model calculations identifies cooperative $^1S_0$ pairing and additional $^3P_2$ attraction as the dominant neutron-neutron contributions to this nonadditive binding, while density cumulants reveal connected four-neutron correlations.

  <summary>Details</summary>

Overall, these findings demonstrate that the internal nuclear structure encoded within NLDs can collectively induce a global redirection of the nucleosynthesis pathway, highlighting the critical need for self-consistent microscopic inputs in future simulations.
