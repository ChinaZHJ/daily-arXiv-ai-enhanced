### [1] [Directional correlations in nuclear charge-radius model residuals enable extrapolation with calibrated uncertainties](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25624)
*Debodyuti Kar,Soumya Bagchi,Timo Dickel,Rituparna Kanungo*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 该论文发现核电荷半径残差沿同位素链呈强各向异性相关，沿同中子素链迅速去相关，并据此提出ARCUS各向异性残差校准方法，使全局模型误差减半，预测区间在盲测试中保持近标称覆盖率，并推广至1008个未测核。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有全局核电荷半径模型（WS*、HFB-25）的残差呈现显著方向性各向异性：沿同位素链持续相关，沿同中子素链快速去相关，且两个残差场高度相关（r≈0.73），表明这是两个模型的共同缺陷，而非单一模型伪影。如何利用这一残差几何结构进行可靠的校准与外推成为核心动机。

Method: 作者提出ARCUS（各向异性残差校准与不确定性缩放）方法：采用各向异性核回归对残差进行校正，并结合经验校准的预测区间。通过留一折交叉验证评估，并在独立的时序盲测试集（129个核）上检验外推校准效果，同时与匹配相同覆盖率的各向同性核进行对比。

Result: ARCUS将WS*和HFB-25的均方根误差降低约两倍；在129个核的盲测试集上，预测区间在推外情况下仍保持接近标称的覆盖率；各向同性核则在该盲集上显著高估不确定性；在52Ca等异常结构区域，ARCUS保持较宽的预测区间以覆盖增大的误差。校准预测扩展到1008个未测量核并按不确定性排序。

Conclusion: 可靠的不确定性校准转移取决于对方向性残差结构的编码。各向异性核校准不仅能显著提升全局模型的精度，还能在不依赖模型特征的情况下保持外推时的真实覆盖率，为未来核电荷半径测量提供排序支持。

Abstract: We identify a pronounced directional anisotropy in the residuals between measured nuclear charge radii and two structurally distinct global models: the phenomenological Weizsäcker--Skyrme formula (WS*) and the microscopic Hartree--Fock--Bogoliubov model (HFB-25). In both cases, the residuals remain correlated over several neutron steps along isotopic chains but decorrelate almost completely after a single proton step along isotonic chains. This common pattern, reinforced by a strong correlation between the two residual fields ($r\simeq0.73$), points to a shared deficiency of both global descriptions rather than a model-specific artefact. Motivated by this geometry, we introduce ARCUS (Anisotropic Residual Calibration with Uncertainty Scaling), which applies an anisotropic kernel-regression correction with empirically calibrated prediction intervals. In out-of-fold cross-validation, ARCUS reduces the root-mean-square errors of both WS* and HFB-25 by approximately a factor of two. On an independent, temporally blind test set of 129 nuclei, its prediction intervals retain coverage close to nominal under extrapolation. An isotropic kernel matched to the same cross-validation coverage instead substantially overestimates uncertainties on the blind set, showing that reliable calibration transfer depends on encoding the directional residual structure. In regions with anomalous structure, such as around $^{52}$Ca, ARCUS keeps its prediction intervals wide enough to cover the increased errors, rather than yielding overconfident point predictions. We also extend these calibrated predictions to 1008 unmeasured nuclei near known isotopic chains, ranked by uncertainty to support the future charge-radius measurements.

### [2] [Evidence for Quartet Binding of Valence Neutrons in $^8$He](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25883)
*Young-Ho Song,Yuan-Zhuo Ma,Dean Lee*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文证明了 ^8He 的四个价中子可实现多模态中子超流的有限系统类比——四中子团簇（quartet），并指出其结合不仅依赖中子间吸引强度，还依赖足够强的吸引配对模式的数量与对称性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 多模态中子超流理论预言四重子是由两个自旋单态 s 波中子对结合而成的束缚态，但此前缺乏有限系统中该四重子结合机制的直接证据；需要阐明有限核系统中四中子非可加结合（nonadditive binding）的物理起源。

Method: 采用 Daejeon16 无芯壳模型（no-core shell model）计算，对中子-中子相互作用进行分波分析，识别协作的 ^1S_0 配对与额外的 ^3P_2 吸引的贡献，并利用密度累积量（density cumulants）揭示关联四中子关联。

Result: 发现 ^1S_0 协作配对与额外的 ^3P_2 吸引是 ^8He 四价中子非可加结合的主要中子-中子贡献来源；泡利阻塞限制了同一对结构的重复使用，而额外的吸引模式提供了额外结合；密度累积量证实了关联的四中子关联的存在。

Conclusion: ^8He 的四个价中子实现了多模态中子超流的有限系统类比，四重子结合同时取决于中子-中子吸引强度以及足够强的吸引配对模式的数量与对称性。

Abstract: Multimodal neutron superfluidity predicts quartets formed as bound states of two spin-singlet $s$-wave neutron pairs. We present evidence that the four valence neutrons of $^8$He realize the finite-system analogue. Quartet binding depends not only on the strength of neutron-neutron attraction but also on the number and symmetry of sufficiently strong attractive pair modes. Pauli blocking limits reuse of the same pair structure, while additional modes can provide extra binding. A partial-wave analysis of the neutron-neutron interaction in Daejeon16 no-core shell-model calculations identifies cooperative $^1S_0$ pairing and additional $^3P_2$ attraction as the dominant neutron-neutron contributions to this nonadditive binding, while density cumulants reveal connected four-neutron correlations.

Motivated by the fact the J = 21/2+ and J =3/2+ states are pure seniority v=3 states for both n=3 and n=5, we compare the energy splitting E(J=21/2+)-E(J=3/2+) for various interactions. We then discuss the position of the lowest J=3/2+ state in both n=3 and n=5, although, this state has not been found experimentally. Calculations with configurations beyond g9/2 are also considered. We next address the fact that the J=21/2+ state is isomeric for both n=3 and n=5.

### [4] [Joint cluster-EFT analysis of $^{16}$N $β$-delayed $α$ spectra and $α$-$^{12}$C scattering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25252)
*Jubin Park,Myeong-Hwan Mun,Shung-Ichi Ando*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文在团簇有效场论框架下重新分析$^{16}$N的β延迟α衰变能谱（Azuma与Tang两组数据），探讨其与$^{12}$C(α,γ)$^{16}$O低能E1跃迁的关联。独立拟合显著改善χ²，但联合拟合与加权策略仍无法同时高质量复现两谱；强参数稳定而弱流系数依赖目标函数，提示未来统一分析需传播实验与模型敏感度。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: $^{16}$N的β延迟α衰变是探测$^{12}$C(α,γ)$^{16}$O低能E1跃迁所需p波α-$^{12}$C连续谱的关键探针，需要检验两组实验能谱能否在同一理论框架下被一致描述，并厘清弱流幅度的不确定性。

Method: 采用团簇有效场论（cluster EFT），将Azuma等人与Tang等人的β延迟α衰变能谱分别独立拟合，并各自与相同的弹性散射数据联合；在此基础上做原始同时拟合与按扇区加权的同时拟合，比较χ²、强参数、归一化比值及拟合弱流系数对不同目标函数的依赖。

Result: 八参数基线独立拟合：Azuma谱χ²_A/N_A=1.732，Tang谱χ²_T/N_T=2.028，明显优于固定传播子情形的4.06与3.56。原始同时拟合为4.319、10.901、6.366（Azuma、Tang、弹性散射），扇区均衡加权后为3.308、8.993、6.466。降低弹性权重改善BDAD扇区但未能恢复独立拟合质量；强参数与归一化比值稳定，弱流系数随目标函数变化。两谱单独兼容共同强连续谱，但最小共同弱流幅度无法同时复现两谱。

Conclusion: 当前数据分析表明两组BDAD能谱与共同的强连续谱分别相容，但无法由单一共同弱流幅度同时描述；拟合的弱流系数对目标函数敏感，说明还需将实验与模型敏感度系统传播到包含辐射俘获的未来统一分析中。

The baseline eight-parameter fits yield $χ^2_{\rm A}/N_{\rm A} = 1.732$ for the Azuma spectrum and $χ^2_{\rm T}/N_{\rm T} = 2.028$ for the Tang spectrum, substantially improving upon the corresponding fixed-propagator values of 4.06 and 3.56, respectively. With sector-balanced weighting, the corresponding values are 3.308, 8.993, and 6.466, respectively. Thus, reducing the relative elastic weight improves the BDAD sectors but does not recover the quality of the independent fits. The common strong parameters and normalization ratio remain stable, whereas the fitted weak-current coefficients depend on the objective. The two spectra are separately compatible with a common strong continuum, but the minimal common weak-current amplitude does not reproduce them simultaneously; its fitted coefficients also depend on the objective. These results define the experimental and model sensitivities that must be propagated in a future unified analysis including radiative capture.

### [5] [Impact of Nuclear Level Density on $r$-Process Rare-Earth Peak Nucleosynthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25440)
*Hang Xu,Peng-Xiang Du,Jian Li,Dong-Liang Fang*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文通过三组天体场景下的r过程模拟，系统比较六种核能级密度（NLD）模型对中子俘获率及核合成的影响，发现微观模型相对唯象模型在NLD上存在系统性偏差，可显著加速稀土峰（A~164）的早期形成并暂时增强其幅度，同时消除r过程对质子奇偶性的依赖，表明NLD内部核结构可整体重定向核合成路径，强调未来模拟需自洽的微观输入。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 稀土峰（A~164）是r过程的显著特征，以往理论研究认为其可能与局域核结构效应有关；但直接反映核结构性质的核能级密度（NLD）相比核质量等性质长期被忽视，缺乏其对核合成影响的系统研究。

Method: 在三种天体物理场景下开展r过程模拟，采用由六种不同NLD模型导出的中子俘获率，系统比较微观模型与唯象模型产生的NLD差异及其对核合成流、稀土峰形成和奇偶敏感性的影响。

Result: 微观模型相对唯象模型在NLD上呈现系统性偏差；偶数A核的NLD系统差异会重定向核流，加速稀土峰的早期形成并暂时增大其幅度；这种底层结构变化还从根本上改变了r过程对中子俘获率的敏感性，消除了其对质子奇偶性的依赖。

Conclusion: NLD中编码的核内部结构可集体性地引发核合成路径的整体重定向，凸显未来r过程模拟中采用自洽微观输入的必要性。

Abstract: The rare-earth peak ($A\sim164$) is a prominent feature of the $r$-process, and previous theoretical studies suggest that it is possibly linked to local nuclear structural effects. However, the nuclear level density (NLD), a physical quantity directly reflecting these properties, has been largely overlooked compared to other structural properties such as nuclear masses. To address this, we perform $r$-process simulations across three astrophysical scenarios using neutron-capture rates derived from six distinct NLD models. Our results reveal that microscopic models yield systematic deviations in NLD relative to phenomenological ones, leading to critical impacts on nucleosynthesis. Specifically, systematic NLD differences in even-$A$ nuclei redirect the nuclear flow, accelerating the early formation of the rare-earth peak and temporarily enhancing its magnitude. This underlying structural shift also fundamentally alters the $r$-process sensitivity to the neutron-capture rate, effectively eliminating its dependence on the odd-even nature of protons. Overall, these findings demonstrate that the internal nuclear structure encoded within NLDs can collectively induce a global redirection of the nucleosynthesis pathway, highlighting the critical need for self-consistent microscopic inputs in future simulations.
