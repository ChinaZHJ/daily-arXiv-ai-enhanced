### [1] [Novel chiral-wobbling excitation mode in atomic nuclei](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09808)
*Kouser Qureshie,S. P. Rouoof,J. A. Sheikh,Nazira Nazir,G. H. Bhat,S. Jehangir,N. A. Rather,R. J. Guo,S. Y. Wang,C. Liu,Q. B. Chen,S. Frauendorf*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文利用三轴投影壳模型（TPSM）和粒子三轴转子（PTR）方法研究 $^{74}$Br 原子核的高自旋带结构，揭示了第三条激发带的独特性质。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 进一步阐明 $^{74}$Br 中已观测到的第三条激发带的性质——此前已确认最低三条带具有手征摇摆（chiral-wobbler）特征，需要弄清第三条激发带的独特结构。

Method: 采用三轴投影壳模型（TPSM）与粒子三轴转子（PTR）两种理论方法进行计算，并对各自的物理量进行分析。

Result: 结果表明，第一条激发带是 yrast 带的手征伙伴带，第二条激发带是基于 yrast 态的摇摆带；分析揭示第三条激发带具有独特性质，被预言为第二条激发带的手征伙伴带，同时又是基于第一条激发带的摇摆模式。

Conclusion: 第三条激发带兼具手征与摇摆双重特征，进一步丰富了 $^{74}$Br 中手征摇摆运动图像。

Abstract: The properties of high-spin band structures in $^{74}$Br nucleus are investigated using the triaxial projected shell model (TPSM) and particle triaxial rotor (PTR) approaches. It has already been demonstrated that lowest three bands observed in $^{74}$Br have a characteristic feature of a chiral-wobbler. The first excited band is the chiral partner of the yrast band and the second excited band is the wobbling band based on the yrast state. We have analyzed the third excited band using the TPSM and PTR calculated quantities, and a unique character of this band is revealed. It is predicted that this band is the chiral partner of the second excited band, and is also a wobbling mode based on the first excited band.

### [2] [Event-by-event net-charge fluctuations in pp, OO, Ne-Ne, and Pb-Pb collisions with ALICE at the LHC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09586)
*Nida Malik*

Main category: nucl-ex

TL;DR: 本文利用ALICE探测器数据，研究pp、OO、Ne-Ne和Pb-Pb碰撞中净电荷的逐事件涨落，通过变量ν_dyn[+,-]及其多重数标度形式，揭示电荷关联随碰撞系统和带电粒子密度的演化规律，并与PYTHIA、EPOS4、AMPT及HIJING等模型对比。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 通过研究守恒量（净电荷、净重子数、奇异数）的逐事件涨落，探究夸克-胶子等离子体（QGP）的性质及强相互作用物质的相图。净电荷涨落对QGP的电荷关联和去禁闭效应敏感，是判别相变和QGP形成的重要探针。

Method: 利用CERN大型强子对撞机（LHC）上ALICE探测器采集的数据，分析pp碰撞（√s = 13和13.6 TeV）以及OO、Ne-Ne、Pb-Pb碰撞（√sNN = 5.36 TeV）中净电荷的逐事件涨落。采用对探测器效率损失具有鲁棒性的变量ν_dyn[+,-]，并考察其在带电粒子密度上的依赖。为消除该变量固有的多重数依赖，以平均多重数进行标度（1/(1/⟨N₊⟩ + 1/⟨N₋⟩)），研究标度后的ν_dyn[+,-]随带电粒子密度的变化。

Result: ν_dyn[+,-]的绝对值随碰撞系统从小到大的增大而平滑减小，且呈现出与能量无关的趋势。ν_dyn[+,-]为负值，表明异号电荷粒子对之间的关联强于同号电荷粒子对之间的关联。标度后的ν_dyn[+,-]随带电粒子密度的依赖关系也被考察。

Conclusion: 净电荷逐事件涨落研究表明，ν_dyn[+,-]的绝对值随碰撞系统尺寸增大而平滑减小且能量无关，负值表明异号电荷关联占主导。结果与PYTHIA、EPOS4、AMPT和HIJING等模型计算进行了比较，为理解不同碰撞系统中电荷关联的产生机制提供了实验约束。

Abstract: Investigations involving the event-by-event fluctuations of conserved quantities, like net charge, net baryon number, and strangeness in heavy-ion collisions, provide insights into the properties of quark-gluon plasma (QGP) and the phase diagram of strongly interacting matter. Event-by-event fluctuations of net electric charge in pp collisions at $\sqrt{s} = 13$ and $13.6$ TeV and for OO, Ne-Ne, and Pb-Pb collisions at $\sqrt{s_{\mathrm{NN}}} = 5.36$ TeV are studied by analysing the data collected by the ALICE detector at CERN Large Hadron Collider. The analysis is carried out in terms of the variable $ν_{\mathrm{dyn}}[+,-]$, chosen for its robustness against detector efficiency losses. The observed dependence of $ν_{\mathrm{dyn}}[+,-]$ on the charged-particle density shows a smooth, energy-independent decrease in the magnitude of $ν_{\mathrm{dyn}}[+,-]$ from smaller to larger collision systems. The negative values of $ν_{\mathrm{dyn}}[+,-]$ indicate a dominance of correlations between oppositely charged particle pairs compared with those arising from like-sign charge pairs. Since $ν_{\mathrm{dyn}}[+,-]$ is known to have an intrinsic dependence on multiplicity, an appropriate scaling is applied. The variable is therefore scaled with the mean multiplicities, $\frac{1}{\frac{1}{\langle N_+ \rangle} + \frac{1}{\langle N_- \rangle}}$. The dependence of the scaled values of $ν_{\mathrm{dyn}}[+,-]$ on charged particle density is also looked into. The results are compared with model calculations from PYTHIA, EPOS4, AMPT and HIJING.

</details>
