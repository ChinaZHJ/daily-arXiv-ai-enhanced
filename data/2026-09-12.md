Method: 作者将形变依赖的有限温度配对理论与多维断点谱（scission-spectrum）分析相结合。在配对猝灭边界附近，对拆散（pair breaking）暴露出近费米面的高$Ω$闯入轨道，并打开高$K$通道；并利用快速颈破裂作为非绝热投影冻结机制进行动力学分析。

Result: 计算得到的最大谱学容量（spectroscopic capacity）范围从几个到十几个 $\hbar$。快速颈破裂表现为非绝热的投影冻结（projection freeze-out），其中配对起动力学闸门作用：配对的临界衰减本身并不产生角动量，而是允许已有的高$K$ 投影以绝热（diabatic）方式保留为局部碎片-$K$ 分量，同时保持总角动量守恒。

Conclusion: 裂变碎片自旋并非由配对衰减直接生成，而是通过配对猝灭使高$K$ 投影在快速断点处被非绝热地保留下来，从而解释了 $^{236}$U 裂变中碎片角动量的来源。

Abstract: The origin of fission-fragment spin remains unresolved. For $^{236}$U fission, we combine deformation-dependent finite-temperature pairing with a multidimensional scission-spectrum analysis. Near the pairing-quenching boundary, pair breaking exposes near-Fermi high-$Ω$ intruder orbitals and opens high-$K$ channels. The resulting maximum spectroscopic capacities range from a few to more than a dozen units of $\hbar$. Rapid neck rupture acts as a non-adiabatic projection freeze-out, where pairing serves as a dynamical gate: its critical attenuation does not generate angular momentum but allows existing high-$K$ projections to be retained diabatically as local fragment-$K$ components while total angular momentum remains conserved.
