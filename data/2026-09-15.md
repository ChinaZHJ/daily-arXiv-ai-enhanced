### [1] [Nuclear landscape based on point-coupling density functional with localized exchange terms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13759)
*Y. N. Huang,Q. Zhao,Y. F. Niu*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文基于协变Hartree-Bogoliubov理论与新发展的PCF-PK1密度泛函，在球近似下探索核素版图，预测7210个束缚核并系统分析基态性质与幻数演化。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在相对论框架下准确描述原子核基态性质并探索核素版图边界；通过引入交换项（Fierz变换）和显式张量耦合来改进密度泛函，进而研究幻数、壳隙及超重核潜在幻数的物理机制。

Method: 采用球近似下的相对论Hartree-Bogoliubov理论，使用新发展的PCF-PK1密度泛函。该泛函通过Fierz变换有效纳入交换项并显式包含张量耦合。通过计算结合能、电荷半径、α衰变能，并与其它泛函及实验对比；利用壳隙和单粒子谱分析壳闭包，研究208Pb之后超重核的潜在幻数及张量耦合对赝自旋对称性的影响。

Result: 预测7210个核为束缚核，结合能均方根偏差为7.170 MeV。消除了Z=58和Z=92处的伪壳闭包；发现208Pb之后超重核的潜在幻数；PCF-PK1中张量耦合有助于恢复赝自旋对称性，使Z=120处的壳闭包减弱。

Conclusion: PCF-PK1密度泛函通过纳入交换项和张量耦合，能合理描述核素版图与基态性质；张量耦合恢复赝自旋对称性并弱化Z=120壳闭包，为超重核幻数结构提供了新认识。

Abstract: Nuclear landscape is initially explored in the framework of relativistic Hartree-Bogoliubov theory under the spherical approximation, adopting the newly developed PCF-PK1 density functional. The functional effectively incorporates exchange terms via the Fierz transformation and explicitly includes the tensor coupling. We analyze the limits of the nuclear landscape and nuclear ground state properties including binding energies, charge radii, α-decay energies, compared with other functionals and experiment. In the present calculations, 7210 nuclei are predicted to be bound with the root-mean-square deviation of binding energies 7.170 MeV. The removal of spurious shell closure at Z = 58 and 92 is discussed by shell gaps and single-particle spectra. For superheavy nuclei, potential magic numbers beyond 208Pb are studied. The inclusion of the tensor coupling in PCF-PK1 helps restore the pseudospin symmetry, leading to a less pronounced shell closure at Z = 120.
