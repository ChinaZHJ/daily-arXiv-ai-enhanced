Abstract: We report the first measurement of the total and t-differential cross sections for near-threshold J/$ψ$ photoproduction on the neutron, obtained with the CLAS12 detector at the Thomas Jefferson National Accelerator Facility. The measurements, performed using a liquid-deuterium target, also provide the incoherent total and t-differential cross sections on the bound proton, enabling the first direct comparison of near-threshold J/$ψ$ photoproduction on bound protons and neutrons. Interpreted within Vector Meson Dominance, holographic QCD, and GPD-based frameworks, the data allow for the investigation of the gluonic structure of bound nucleons. A comparison with free proton results reveals hints of modifications to the gluon structure of nucleons in the nuclear medium, within the assumptions of the extraction and on the underlying production mechanism. The comparison of bound neutron and bound proton cross sections provides new constraints on the near-threshold J/$ψ$ production mechanism, which is crucial in order to establish J/$ψ$ photoproduction as a probe of the nucleon's gluonic structure.

### [9] [Measurement of the forward angle $^{12}$C+$^{12}$C fragmentation differential cross sections at 62 MeV/nucleon](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03636)
*G. Guo,G. Casini,B. H. Sun,S. Barlini,A. Camaiani,C. Frosin,I. Lombardo,O. Lopez,S. Piantelli,I. Tanihata,S. Terashima,S. Valdré,G. Verde,F. W. Zhao,L. Baldesi,B. Borderie,R. Bougault,C. Ciampi,I. TL;DR: 使用FAZIA阵列在62 MeV/核子下对12C+12C反应进行了前向角小角度碎片微分截面的高精度测量，提取了Z=1–6碎片在2°≤θlab≤8°的角分布，并与Goldhaber及Van Bibber模型及既往数据进行了系统比较。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 深入理解中能重离子碰撞中碎片的产生机制与前向角角分布特征，特别是耗散过程与弹核碎裂机制的相对贡献，并检验不同理论模型（Goldhaber与Van Bibber）对实验角分布的描述能力。

Method: 利用FAZIA阵列在62 MeV/核子条件下测量12C+12C反应；采用ΔE-E技术与脉冲形状分析（PSA）相结合进行粒子鉴别，并执行精确的能量刻度；提取Z=1到6碎片在2°≤θlab≤8°范围内的微分截面角分布；将实验数据与Goldhaber模型、Van Bibber模型及50和95 MeV/核子的已有数据（通过运动学标度）进行比较。

Result: 对较重碎片，Van Bibber公式比Goldhaber模型更好地描述角分布，表明在所测角度范围内耗散过程的宽分量占主导；α粒子呈现异常窄的角分布，可能源于12C的内禀团簇结构；与同入射能的已有数据符合很好且本文报道的粒子种类更完整；提出运动学标度以与50及95 MeV/核子的既往实验数据进行比较。

Conclusion: 该高精度测量表明前向角小角度碎片角分布主要由耗散过程主导，Van Bibber模型对较重碎片描述更佳；α粒子的异常窄分布可归因于12C的团簇结构。结果与已有数据一致，并通过运动学标度实现了跨能量比较，为核反应碎裂机制提供了更系统的实验约束。

Abstract: The present work reports on high-precision measurements of forward-angle fragmentation differential cross sections for the [12]C +[12] C reaction at 62 MeV/nucleon using the FAZIA array. Angular distributions for fragments from Z = 1 to 6 were extracted in the range 2 <= theta_lab <= 8 degrees. Particle identification was achieved by combining the Delta E - E technique with Pulse Shape Analysis, and precise energy calibrations were performed. The results show that for heavier fragments, the angular distributions are better described by the Van Bibber formulation than by the Goldhaber model, consistent with the dominance of a wide component from dissipative processes in the measured angular range. Notably, alpha particles exhibit an anomalously narrow angular distribution, likely originating from the intrinsic cluster structure of [12]C. Comparisons with existing data at the same incident energy show very good agreement, with a more complete set of species reported here, while a kinematic scaling is proposed to compare our data with previous experimental data at 50 and 95 MeV/nucleon.

### [10] [Neutron dynamics in the fusion of neutron-rich O ions with $^{12}$C](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03914)
*A. Leon,H. Desilets,Rohit Kumar,J. Samol,Z. Berend,R. T. deSouza,C. Ciampi,D. Ackermann,A. Chbihi,M. Basson,S. Brown,K. W. Brown,J. Cory,G. Flores,C. E. McCormick,V. Zerbach,C. Dembski,P. D. O'Malley,W. W. von Seeger,K. Godbey,A. S. Umar*

Main category: nucl-ex

TL;DR: 首次测量了 21O + 12C 的熔合激发函数，并将其与中子数较少的同位素体系进行对比，考察 d5/2 壳层价中子对熔合势垒的影响，并用 DCFHF 与 DCTDHF 理论评估动力学效应。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探究中子过剩（尤其是 d5/2 壳层价中子）如何影响重离子熔合反应的激发函数、势垒高度 VB 与势垒位置 RB，从而理解价中子对熔合机制的贡献。

Method: 首次实验测量 21O + 12C 的熔合激发函数，并与中子数较少的氧同位素体系进行系统比较，提取势垒高度 VB 与势垒位置 RB 随中子过剩的变化；将实验数据与密度约束冻结 Hartree-Fock（DCFHF）理论和密度约束含时 Hartree-Fock（DCTDHF）理论两种模型进行对比以分离动力学效应。

Result: 实验数据明确显示所提取的势垒高度 VB 和势垒位置 RB 对中子过剩存在清晰的依赖性；通过与 DCFHF 和 DCTDHF 理论的比较，可用于判断动力学过程在熔合中的作用。

Conclusion: d5/2 壳层价中子对熔合激发函数及势垒特征具有显著影响，中子过剩导致势垒高度与位置的系统性变化，动力学效应在解释实验熔合数据时不可忽略。

Abstract: The fusion excitation function for $^{21}$O + $^{12}$C was measured for the first time and compared to the fusion of less neutron-rich isotopes. The impact of valence neutrons in the d$_{5/2}$ shell on the fusion excitation function is examined. The experimental data manifest a clear dependence of the extracted barrier height, V$_B$, and barrier position, R$_B$ on neutron excess. To assess the role of dynamics the experimental data are compared with both density constrained frozen Hartree Fock (DCFHF) and density constrained time-dependent Hartree Fock (DCTDHF) theories.
