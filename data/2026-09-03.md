### [1] [Spin transport in intermediate-energy heavy-ion collisions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01994)
*Jun Xu,Bao-An Li*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本综述简要报告了在中等能量重离子碰撞中，通过模拟自旋-同位旋依赖的Boltzmann-Uehling-Uhlenbeck（SIBUU）输运方程的测试粒子法解来研究自旋输运现象的现状，总结了主要结果并指出进一步研究的关键挑战。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 利用中等能量重离子碰撞研究稠密富中子介质中核自旋-轨道势所关联的新物理，推动自旋输运现象的理论与实验研究。

Method: 采用测试粒子法数值求解自旋-同位旋依赖的Boltzmann-Uehling-Uhlenbeck（SIBUU）输运方程，通过自旋和同位旋相关的碰撞项与平均场描述自旋输运过程。

Result: 展示了核子的全局与局域自旋极化、核子集体流的自旋劈裂、不同自旋态轻团簇流等观测量可用于探测稠密富中子介质中的自旋-轨道势；自旋-轨道势和严格的角动量守恒也会影响自旋平均的观测量。

Conclusion: SIBUU框架为中等能量重离子碰撞中的自旋输运提供了有效研究工具，新发现与挑战有望激励相关的理论与实验进一步探索。

Abstract: In this mini-review, we provide a brief status report on investigating spin transport phenomena in intermediate-energy heavy-ion collisions by simulating solutions of the spin- and isospin-dependent Boltzmann-Uehling-Uhlenbeck (SIBUU) transport equation using the test-particle method. We outline the physics foundation and technical approach, summarize our main results and identify key challenges in further studying spin transport within the SIBUU framework. As examples, we show how the global and local spin polarizations of nucleons, the spin splitting of nucleon collective flows as well as flows of light clusters at different spin states can be used to explore interesting new physics associated with the nuclear spin-orbit potential in dense neutron-rich medium. The spin-orbit potential and the rigorous angular momentum conservation are also shown to affect spin-averaged observables. Hopefully the new findings and challenges identified here will stimulate further studies both theoretically and experimentally on spin transport in intermediate-energy heavy-ion collisions.

### [2] [Halo-EFT: an effective and efficient tool to study reactions with halo nuclei](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02704)
*Quentin Bozet,Live-Palm Kubushishi,Pierre Capel*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文提出将晕核有效场论（Halo-EFT）纳入现有反应计算程序，用于分析晕核（如19C和11Be）的库仑碎裂实验数据，该方法高效且有效。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 晕核因其价核子与核芯的弱束缚而呈现异常大的物质半径和团簇结构，且寿命短，主要通过碎裂等反应来研究。现有反应分析需要一种既能准确描述晕核结构、又能高效对接实验数据的理论工具。

Method: 将晕核有效场论（Halo-EFT）嵌入现有的反应计算程序。具体分两步：首先结合贝叶斯方法研究19C的库仑碎裂；其次扩展Halo-EFT以包含核芯激发，检验11Be库仑碎裂对谱因子（spectroscopic factors）的敏感性。

Result: 该方法能够有效且高效地分析晕核的实验反应数据，例如19C的库仑碎裂可通过贝叶斯方法约束，而11Be的库仑碎裂可用于检验核芯激发引入的谱因子的影响。

Conclusion: Halo-EFT与反应程序结合为分析晕核碎裂实验数据提供了一套有效而高效的框架，并可推广至包含核芯激发的情形，从而提升对谱因子等结构参数的约束能力。

Abstract: Halo nuclei are exotic nuclei, which exhibit a much larger matter radius than their isobars. This unusual size is now understood as a threshold effect, in which one or two valence nucleons are loosely bound to the core of the nucleus. Thanks to this loose binding, halo nuclei exhibit a strongly clusterised structure with a diffuse neutron halo surrounding a compact core. Being short lived, halo nuclei are mostly studied through reactions, such as breakup. In this talk we show that including Halo Effective Field Theory within existing reaction codes is both effective and efficient to analyse experimental data. We first apply this idea to study the Coulomb breakup of 19C within a Bayesian approach. Second, we extend Halo-EFT to include the core's excitation to test the sensitivity of the Coulomb breakup of 11Be on spectroscopic factors.

### [3] [Unveiling QCD Criticality with Cross-Rapidity Net-Baryon Cumulants](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01859)
*Jianing Li,Shuzhe Shi,Lipei Du*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文提出利用两个分离快度窗口之间的净重子涨落关联来区分全局重子数守恒的信号与QCD临界点的临界涨落信号。守恒导致负的跨窗口关联，而长波长临界涨落导致正的关联；在正则独立源框架下二者在领头阶可加，故可估计并扣除守恒背景，从而在束能扫描实验中更清晰地搜寻临界性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 守恒荷涨落是寻找QCD临界端点的首要工具，但其束能依赖受到全局重子数守恒的干扰，且该影响随实验接受度覆盖碰撞系统的比例而变化，难以单纯从单窗口涨落中分离临界信号，因此需要新方法区分守恒背景与临界动力学信号。

Method: 作者提出使用两个分离快度窗口之间净重子涨落的关联（cross-rapidity cumulants）。在正则独立源框架内，全局守恒产生负的跨窗口关联，而共同的、长波长的临界涨落产生正的关联；二者在领头阶可加，因此可估计并扣除守恒-诱导的背景项。进一步构造按产额标度（yield-scaled）的关联量，并沿一条经过假设临界端点的冻结轨迹考察其对Ising映射平衡关联长度的响应。

Result: 结果表明，扣除领头守恒背景后，产额标度的关联量会跟随Ising映射的平衡关联长度沿经过临界端点的冻结轨迹呈现非单调增强；较宽的快度窗口会通过热抹平（thermal smearing）效应抑制该响应。

Conclusion: 跨快度累积量提供了一种快度微分的策略，可有效降低领头守恒背景，从而锐化束能扫描实验中基于涨落的QCD临界性搜寻。

Abstract: Fluctuations of conserved charges are a primary tool in the search for the QCD critical endpoint, but their beam-energy dependence is complicated by global baryon-number conservation, whose influence changes as the experimental acceptance covers different fractions of the collision system. We propose using correlations of net-baryon fluctuations between two separated rapidity windows to exploit the distinct signatures of conservation and critical dynamics. Global conservation produces a negative cross-window correlation, whereas a common long-wavelength critical fluctuation produces a positive one. We show that, within a canonical independent-source framework, the conservation-induced background and the critical signal enter additively at leading order, allowing the leading conservation term to be estimated and subtracted. The resulting yield-scaled correlator follows the nonmonotonic enhancement of an Ising-mapped equilibrium correlation length along a freeze-out trajectory passing near a hypothetical critical endpoint, while wider rapidity windows reduce the response through thermal smearing. Cross-rapidity cumulants therefore provide a rapidity-differential strategy for reducing the leading conservation background and sharpening fluctuation-based searches for QCD criticality in beam-energy-scan experiments.

### [4] [Impact of nuclear triaxial deformation on electromagnetic fields in relativistic $^{129}\mathrm{Xe}+^{129}\mathrm{Xe}$ collisions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02053)
*Maidi Huang,Jin Hu,Yunpeng Liu,Baoyi Chen*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 利用 Liénard–Wiechert 势与三轴形变 Woods–Saxon 密度，研究 Xe+Xe 碰撞中三轴角 γ 对初始电磁场分布的影响，发现核三轴形变会导致横平面电磁场的空间重排，表明初始电磁场编码了核内禀形状信息。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 相对论重离子碰撞产生的电磁场高度依赖于核电荷的初始空间分布，而核的三轴形变（triaxiality）对电磁场的影响尚未被系统考察，需探索电磁场能否作为探测原子核内禀形变的额外手段。

Method: 采用三轴形变的 Woods–Saxon 核密度分布，结合 Liénard–Wiechert 势，计算 √s_NN=5.44 TeV 的 ¹²⁹Xe+¹²⁹Xe 半中心碰撞在碰撞时刻（t=0）的横向电、磁场分布，并以球形核碰撞为基线，系统考察三轴角 γ 对场结构的影响。

Result: 核三轴形变导致横平面上电场与磁场出现明显的空间重分布，其结构随三轴角 γ 改变而显著变化，与球形核基线的场分布存在清晰差异。

Conclusion: 初始电磁场编码了原子核内禀形状的关键信息，可作为对核形变及其对场敏感观测量影响的额外约束手段。

Abstract: Electromagnetic fields produced in relativistic heavy-ion collisions depend sensitively on the initial spatial distribution of nuclear charge. Using the Liénard--Wiechert potential with a triaxially deformed Woods--Saxon density, we calculate the transverse electric and magnetic field distributions in $^{129}\mathrm{Xe}+{}^{129}\mathrm{Xe}$ collisions at $\sqrt{s_{NN}}=5.44\text{ TeV}$. We systematically examine how the triaxiality angle $γ$ modifies the field structure at the collision time ($t=0$) in semi-central events, using spherical nuclear collisions as a baseline. The results show that nuclear triaxiality causes distinct spatial redistributions of both electric and magnetic fields in the transverse plane. These findings indicate that initial electromagnetic fields encode key information on intrinsic nuclear shapes, offering an additional constraint on nuclear deformation and its consequences for field-sensitive observables in heavy-ion collisions.

### [5] [Effects of Strong Magnetic Fields on the Equation of State and Mass-Radius Structure of Hyperonic Neutron-Star Matter with Anomalous Magnetic Moments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02441)
*W. German,T. Mello,J. P. W. Diener,G. C. Hillhouse*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文在相对论平均场（RMF）框架下研究强磁场对冷、电荷中性、β平衡的超子中子星物质的状态方程（EoS）和星体结构的影响。研究纳入了完整重子八重态与轻子，带电粒子被朗道量子化，所有重子通过反常磁矩（AMM）与磁场耦合。采用FSU2H超子参数化，对比了零磁场、恒定磁场和密度相关磁场三种情形。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 强磁场普遍存在于中子星（尤其是磁星）中，但其对含超子的致密物质状态方程及星体结构的影响尚不完全清楚，特别是反常磁矩（AMM）耦合在这一过程中扮演的角色缺乏系统基准研究。因此需要量化朗道量子化与AMM自旋劈裂的竞争效应，并检验其对观测约束（2.00–2.02太阳质量的中子星及小半径）的兼容性。

Method: 在相对论平均场框架下，物质部分包含完整重子八重态与轻子；带电粒子进行朗道量子化处理，所有重子通过其反常磁矩与磁场耦合。采用FSU2H超子参数化进行计算，并对零磁场、恒定磁场以及密度相关磁场剖面三种情形进行比较，分析强磁场通过朗道量子化与AMM诱导自旋劈裂的竞争作用对超子组分、磁化、状态方程及质量-半径结构的影响。

Result: 强磁场通过朗道量子化与AMM诱导自旋劈裂的竞争效应改变了超子组分。朗道量子化使磁化超子物质的状态方程变软；而纳入AMM则提供了额外的磁致硬化机制。无论是否包含AMM耦合，计算结果均与2.00–2.02倍太阳质量的中子星观测及小半径约束保持一致。

Abstract: We investigate the effects of strong magnetic fields on the equation of state (EoS) and stellar structure of cold, charge-neutral, $β$-equilibrated hyperonic neutron-star matter within a relativistic mean-field (RMF) framework. The matter sector contains the full baryon octet and leptons, while charged particles are Landau quantized and all baryons are coupled to the magnetic field through their anomalous magnetic moments (AMM). The calculation is performed with the RMF FSU2H hyperonic parameterization and compared for zero field, constant magnetic fields, and density-dependent magnetic-field profiles. We find that strong magnetic fields modify the hyperonic composition through the competing effects of Landau quantization and AMM-induced spin splitting. Landau quantization softens the magnetized hyperonic equation of state. The inclusion of AMM provides an additional magnetic stiffening mechanism in hyperonic matter. The results for the inclusion of the AMM coupling and not are still consistent with observations of $2.00$-$2.02\,M_{\odot}$ neutron stars and small radii. The present work therefore provides a benchmark for assessing the influence of AMM on the composition, magnetization, equation of state, and mass-radius structure of magnetized hyperonic neutron-star matter.

### [6] [Clustered Nature of Hot and Dense Nuclear Matter: A quantum statistical approach](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02463)
*G. Röpke,H. Pais,J. B. Natowitz,D. Blaschke*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文比较了量子统计方法与相空间排斥体积方法，用于计算热核物质在饱和密度附近轻团簇（²H、³H、³He、⁴He）的平衡丰度，发现两种方法在团簇丰度随密度趋于饱和时均出现急剧下降，与相对论平均场计算结果一致，并指出需考虑非平衡效应以研究重离子碰撞中的团簇形成。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 热核物质在饱和密度附近轻团簇的平衡丰度对核物理和天体物理应用至关重要，但不同理论方法给出的结果存在分歧，因此需要厘清各方法的核心要素并评估其一致性。

Method: 作者对量子统计方法与相空间排斥体积方法进行了比较，重点分析了两者的主要成分——莫特动量（Mott momentum）以及轻团簇的动量分布函数；并将结果与相对论平均场计算进行对照。

Conclusion: 作者概述了改进确定热稠密物质热力学平衡组成的可能途径，并强调在研究重离子碰撞中的团簇形成时必须考虑非平衡效应。

Abstract: The equilibrium abundances of the light clusters $^2$H, $^3$H, $^3$He, $^4$He in hot nuclear matter at densities near the saturation density are of essential interest for nuclear physics and astrophysical applications, but theoretical approaches give diverging answers. We compare the quantum statistical approach with the recently discussed phase-space excluded-volume approach. We analyze the main ingredients, the Mott momentum, and the momentum distribution functions of light clusters. We observe a sharp decrease in cluster abundances as the density approaches saturation density, that is also seen in a relativistic mean-field calculation. We outline possible improvements in determining the composition of hot, dense matter in thermodynamic equilibrium. Non-equilibrium effects must be taken into account to investigate cluster formation in heavy-ion collisions.
