Result: $\mathcal{O}(α^2)$ 阶的矩阵元较大，对 $V_{ud}$ 提取的影响达到 $10^{-4}$ 量级；对 ${}^{10}\mathrm{C}$，$\mathcal{O}(αε_χ^2)$ 阶三核子算符的矩阵元对 $δ_{\mathrm{NS}}$ 提供约 2% 的修正，超出了当前精度目标。中等质量核中三核子修正的相对大小（其中两体贡献往往较小）仍有待研究。

Conclusion: 三核子辐射修正在 $V_{ud}$ 的高精度确定中不可忽略：$\mathcal{O}(α^2)$ 项效应显著，而 $\mathcal{O}(αε_χ^2)$ 项对 ${}^{10}\mathrm{C}$ 的 $δ_{\mathrm{NS}}$ 贡献约 2%，已超出当前精度要求。未来需进一步考察中等质量核中三核子修正的大小，以获得更可靠的 $V_{ud}$ 值。

Abstract: We derive three-nucleon potentials for radiative corrections at order $\mathcal{O}(αε_χ^2)$ in chiral effective field theory where $α$ is the electromagnetic coupling and $ε_χ$ is the chiral expansion parameter. These corrections contribute to the nuclear-structure-dependent term $δ_\mathrm{NS}$ for determinations of the Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix element $V_{ud}$ from superallowed nuclear $β$ decays. We compute matrix elements of three-nucleon contributions at orders $\mathcal{O}(α^2)$ and $\mathcal{O}(αε_χ^2)$ using quantum Monte Carlo methods. We find the matrix elements at order $\mathcal{O}(α^2)$ to be large and to have an effect on the extraction of $V_{ud}$ at the scale of $10^{-4}$. In the case of $^{10}$C, the matrix elements of $\mathcal{O}(αε_χ^2)$ three-nucleon operators provide a 2% correction to $δ_{\text{NS}}$, and are thus beyond the current precision goals. The relative size of three-nucleon corrections to $δ_{\text{NS}}$ in medium mass nuclei, where two-body contributions tend to be smaller, remains to be investigated. We then use these results to determine $V_{ud}$ from the superallowed transition ${}^{10}\mathrm{C}\rightarrow{}^{10}\mathrm{B}$.
