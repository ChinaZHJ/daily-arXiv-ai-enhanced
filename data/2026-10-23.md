We determined the half-life of $^{229\mathrm{m}}$Th$^+$ to be 0.46(8) s. Our result differs by several orders of magnitude from the half-lives of internal conversion and radiative decay, indirectly suggesting the existence of the electronic bridge decay of $^{229\mathrm{m}}$Th. This will enable the direct observation and further investigation of the electronic bridge process, which will contribute to understanding nuclear-electron interactions and accelerating nuclear deexcitation in the operation of a thorium-based nuclear clock.
