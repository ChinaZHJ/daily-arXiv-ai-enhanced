### [1] [Proton emission half-lives and shape coexistence for $71 \leq Z \leq 83$ odd-$Z$ nuclei](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19601)
*Yongbeom Choi,Chang-Hwan Lee,Youngman Kim*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文利用 WKB 近似，结合变形相对论连续谱 Hartree-Bogoliubov 理论（DRHBc）与相对论连续谱 Hartree-Bogoliubov 理论（RCHB）给出的核势，研究了 71≤Z≤83 奇 Z 核单质子发射体的半衰期，发现四极形变对半衰期的预测改善有限，光谱因子是主要影响因素，并讨论了形变共存（如 ¹⁷⁰Au）对半衰期的影响。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 单质子发射是质子滴线附近核结构的直接探针，对理解奇异衰变模式与核合成过程至关重要。作者希望检验四极形变（特别是通过 DRHBc 引入的形变）对单质子发射体半衰期预测的影响，并厘清形变、光谱因子与衰变宽度在半衰期中的相对贡献。

Method: 作者采用 WKB 近似计算单质子发射半衰期，核势分别取自 DRHBc 和 RCHB 理论。通过对比两种理论框架下的计算结果与现有实验数据，评估四极形变的作用；进一步分析光谱因子与衰变宽度对半衰期的贡献，并针对形变共存核（如 ¹⁷⁰Au）讨论形变通过光谱因子对半衰期的影响。

Result: 研究表明：1) 通过 DRHBc 引入的四极形变对变形核半衰期预测的改善十分有限；2) 所有研究核素的基态均有 |β₂,DRHBc| < 0.15，在此有限形变范围内，与衰变宽度相比，光谱因子对半衰期起主导作用；3) 对存在形变共存的核（如 ¹⁷⁰Au），半衰期随四极形变显著变化，且该变化主要源于光谱因子受形变的影响；4) 计算半衰期并非由共存极小间的总能差决定，而是受形变影响的光谱因子支配。

Conclusion: 在研究的 71≤Z≤83 奇 Z 核中，四极形变对半衰期的预测改善有限，光谱因子而非衰变宽度是决定半衰期的主导因素。形变共存（如 ¹⁷⁰Au）通过光谱因子对半衰期产生显著影响，说明在预测单质子发射半衰期时应重点关注形变对光谱因子的调制作用，而非总能差。

Abstract: One-proton emission is a direct probe of nuclear structure near the proton drip line and plays a critical role in understanding exotic decay modes and nucleosynthesis processes. In this study, we investigate the half-lives of one-proton emitters for $71 \leq Z \leq 83$ odd-$Z$ nuclei by employing the WKB approximation with nuclear potentials obtained from the deformed relativistic Hartree-Bogoliubov theory in continuum (DRHBc) and, for comparison, the relativistic continuum Hartree-Bogoliubov theory (RCHB). We first compare the calculated half-lives with available experimental data. The inclusion of quadrupole deformation via the DRHBc hardly contributes to improving the predictions of half-lives for the deformed nuclei. We find that all the studied nuclei exhibit ground states with $|β_{2,{\rm DRHBc}}| < 0.15$, and within this limited deformation range the spectroscopic factor provides the dominant contribution to the half-life, compared to the decay width. In particular, for nuclei exhibiting shape coexistence in DRHBc, such as $^{170}$Au, where the half-life varies significantly with the quadrupole deformation through its effect on the spectroscopic factor, we expect shape coexistence to exert a substantial influence on the variation of half-lives. Finally, we discuss the half-lives in consideration of shape coexistence. Our results indicate that the calculated half-life is governed not by the total-energy difference between coexisting minima but rather by the spectroscopic factor influenced by the deformation.

### [2] [Size and Distance Scales in the Nucleon and in Dense Baryonic Matter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19792)
*Wolfram Weise*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文是一篇纪念 Mannque Rho 的献文，回顾并更新了他关于核子低能结构的"双尺度"图景，进而探讨其在致密冷重子物质（中子星核心）中的含义，并与中子星观测量的贝叶斯推断结果相对照。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 纪念 Mannque Rho 数十年来持续启发和塑造的一系列思想与研究方向，梳理从核子低能双尺度结构到中子星核心致密冷重子物质的学术脉络，并检验这些早期概念在当代研究中的生命力。

Method: 文章沿思想史线索展开：首先回顾早期的开创性概念，再结合近期对核子"核心"（core）与"云"（cloud）尺寸的先进分析对这些概念进行更新，随后讨论其对致密核物质的含义，并通过中子星观测量的贝叶斯推断分析与经验信息相对照。

Result: 研究将历史概念与当代分析相衔接，表明核子的双尺度（核心与云）图景对理解致密核物质性质仍具重要价值，并与中子星观测数据的经验约束相互印证或检验。

Conclusion: Mannque Rho 所倡导的核子双尺度图景及其对致密重子物质的延伸至今仍具解释力，为理解中子星核心物质提供了一个持续有效的理论框架。

Abstract: This memorial tribute to Mannque Rho follows a line of thoughts and ideas that he continuously inspired and shaped over many decades: from the two-scales picture of low-energy nucleon structure to dense and cold baryonic matter as it is realized in the cores of neutron stars. Early groundbreaking concepts are recalled and updated by recent advanced analyses of the `core' and `cloud' sizes of the nucleon. Implications for dense nuclear matter are then discussed and confronted with empirical information from Bayesian inference analyses of neutron star observables.

### [3] [Correspondence between hydrodynamic frames, transport coefficients, and hydrodynamic modes in relativistic fluids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20324)
*Md Hasanujjaman,Mahfuzur Rahaman*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文推导了相对论耗散流体动力学中Eckart帧与Landau-Lifshitz帧运输系数间的精确变换关系，证明物理可观测量（如声衰减系数）具有帧不变性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 不同流体动力学帧的选择会直接影响耗散流体动力学中运输系数的数值，导致不同来源（重离子碰撞、格点QCD、动力学理论）的系数难以比较。作者旨在澄清帧依赖性的本质，并建立两帧间的精确变换关系。

Method: 作者推导Eckart帧与Landau-Lifshitz帧之间的精确变换，发现两者热导率由一个依赖于焓的因子相联系。采用富重子的相对论流体（玻尔兹曼核子气体状态方程）进行数值验证，并通过声传播的线性化分析检验物理可观测量的帧不变性。

Result: Landau-Lifshitz热导率相对于Eckart热导率被抑制，且差异随温度和重子化学势增大而增大。声衰减系数在两帧中完全相同，证实物理可观测量具有帧不变性。

Conclusion: 运输系数的帧依赖性仅反映耗散效应分解为热流与扩散流的方式不同，底层输运物理并未改变。因此，在比较重离子碰撞、格点QCD与动力学理论计算的运输系数时，必须明确指定所用的流体动力学帧。

Abstract: The choice of hydrodynamic frame directly influences the numerical values of transport coefficients in relativistic dissipative hydrodynamics. We derive the exact transformation between the Eckart and Landau--Lifshitz frames and show that their thermal conductivities are related by an enthalpy-dependent factor. Using a baryon-rich relativistic fluid described by a Boltzmann nucleon gas equation of state, we find that the Landau--Lifshitz thermal conductivity is suppressed relative to the Eckart conductivity, with the difference increasing with temperature and baryon chemical potential. A linearized analysis of sound propagation demonstrates that the sound attenuation coefficient remains identical in both frames, confirming the frame invariance of physical observables. Our results show that the frame dependence of transport coefficients reflects only the different decomposition of dissipative effects into heat-flow and diffusion currents, while the underlying transport physics remains unchanged. These findings underscore the importance of specifying the hydrodynamic frame when comparing transport coefficients from heavy-ion collisions, lattice QCD, and kinetic-theory calculations.
