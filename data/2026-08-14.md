### [1] [Relativistic dynamical effects in proton emission: the Wentzel-Kramers-Brillouin method for 1+1 dimensional Dirac equation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12767)
*Guangping Chen,Wenmin Deng,Ganlong Ding,Sibo Wang,Jing Peng,Haozhao Liang*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文从1+1维Dirac方程出发，利用WKB近似推导出相对论穿透概率，指出半经典动量应由Schrödinger等效势U_eff(r)（含标量势、矢量势及其平方差项）决定，而非简单采用S(r)+V(r)。将U_eff用于质子发射计算后，系统地降低了穿透概率与碰撞频率、增大了半衰期，且该相对论动力学效应随轨道角动量增大而增强，在^144Tm中可达约84%。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 以往相对论量子隧穿研究中普遍采用标量与矢量势的简单求和S(r)+V(r)来描述有效势，这种处理忽略了标量势与矢量势耦合带来的动力学修正。作者旨在从Dirac方程严格推导更准确的等效势，并量化相对论动力学效应对质子发射的影响。

Method: 以1+1维Dirac方程为基础，运用WKB近似推导相对论穿透概率；得到半经典动量由Schrödinger等效势U_eff(r)=S(r)+(E/m)V(r)+[S²(r)-V²(r)]/(2m)定义；随后将该等效势与传统的S(r)+V(r)分别代入质子发射计算，比较两者给出的穿透概率、碰撞频率和半衰期。

Result: 采用U_eff(r)后，穿透概率和碰撞频率被系统性压低，进而使预测的半衰期增大；该相对论动力学效应随轨道角动量增大而更加显著，^144Tm的半衰期增幅可达约84%。

Conclusion: 正确的相对论等效势应包含标量势、矢量势的耦合修正项（U_eff），而非简单的S(r)+V(r)求和；这一修正对质子发射等相对论量子隧穿过程的半衰期具有不可忽略的影响，尤其在高角动量情形下。

Abstract: Starting from the $1+1$ dimensional (one spatial and one temporal dimension) Dirac equation, we employ the Wentzel-Kramers-Brillouin (WKB) approximation to derive the corresponding relativistic penetration probability. The derivation shows that the semiclassical momentum is determined by the Schrödinger-equivalent potential $ U_{\text{eff}}(r) = S(r) + \frac{E}{m}V(r) + \frac{S^{2}(r)-V^{2}(r)}{2m}$, instead of the simple sum of scalar and vector potentials $S(r)+V(r)$, which has been adopted widely in the studies of relativistic quantum tunneling. We then quantify the relativistic dynamical effects in proton emission by comparing the results obtained with $U_{\text{eff}}(r)$ and those obtained with $S(r)+V(r)$. Incorporating $U_{\text{eff}}(r)$ systematically reduces the penetration probability and the assault frequency, and consequently increases the predicted half-life. The relativistic dynamical effect becomes more pronounced with higher orbital angular momentum and can reach about $84\%$ in the half-life of $^{144}\mathrm{Tm}$.

### [2] [Parametric Matrix Models for Emulation in Nuclear and Many-Body Physics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12837)
*Patrick Cook*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本论文提出了一种名为参数矩阵模型（PMMs）的仿真模拟（emulation）与通用机器学习框架，它结合了传统降基方法与现代参数化机器学习，并开发了开源软件包 pyPMM，用于高效构建和部署核物理与多体物理领域的代理模型。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 核物理与多体物理的进步依赖于求解大规模强关联量子多体问题，但理论模型日益复杂且计算代价高昂；同时，量化模型预测的不确定性与拟合实验参数需要反复调用这些昂贵模型。为此需要代理模型（emulators）来降低计算成本并实现不确定性量化与参数拟合。

Method: 提出参数矩阵模型（PMMs）这一通用机器学习与仿真模拟框架，融合传统降基方法（reduced basis method）与现代参数化机器学习。论文发展了 PMMs 的形式化数学框架，并给出逐步应用流程；同时开发了开源、模块化、可扩展且经 GPU 优化的 pyPMM 软件包用于模型的构建、训练、共享与部署。

Result: PMMs 能够根据需求保留底层物理系统任意程度的物理信息，从而在提供优异性能的同时，兼具近乎无与伦比的适应性、可解释性与可信度。pyPMM 包使任何研究者都能构建、训练、共享和部署基于 PMM 的仿真模拟器，论文中所有 PMM 示例均由该包生成。

Conclusion: 该论文系统梳理了核物理与多体物理中仿真模拟方法的现状，并展示了许多方法在数学上的紧密关联及为优化特定性质而做的取舍；核心贡献是 PMMs 框架及其配套的 pyPMM 软件包，为大规模强关联量子多体问题提供了一种高性能、灵活且可信的仿真模拟工具。

Abstract: Progress in nuclear and many-body physics today is predicated on the ability to solve large-scale, strongly correlated quantum many-body problems. As the theoretical models become more sophisticated, they also become more computationally complex. Simultaneously, quantifying uncertainty in model predictions and fitting free parameters to experimental observations requires repeated evaluation of these expensive models. Surrogate models---known as emulators---provide the means of accomplishing these goals.
  This thesis provides an introduction into the current state of emulation in nuclear and many-body physics. The motivations, goals, and origins of currently popular emulation methods are discussed along with selected examples. We see how many methods are closely mathematically related and how trade-offs are made to optimize specific properties or applications.
  The central work in this thesis is the method of parametric matrix models (PMMs), an emulation and general machine learning framework which combines aspects of traditional reduced basis method with modern parametric machine learning. PMMs are able to retain as much or as little physical information about the underlying system as desired, yielding not only excellent performance but also nearly unparalleled adaptability, interpretability, and trustworthiness as an emulation method.
  A formal mathematical framework for PMMs is developed and accompanied by practical step-by-step procedures for the application of the method.
  As part of this thesis, the open-source pyPMM package was developed. This package enables any researcher to construct, train, share, and deploy PMM-based emulators with modular, extendable, and graphics processing unit (GPU)-optimized code. All PMM examples in this thesis were created using this package.

### [3] [Effective field theory of quasi-hydrodynamics from kinetic theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13542)
*Lorenzo Gavassino*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文从动力学理论出发，为线性准流体力学建立了一套严格的等效场论框架，证明以色列-斯图尔特型方程是慢弛豫模式的普适描述，且高阶修正继承对称性、昂萨格、正定性与因果性约束。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 准流体力学描述具有准守恒自由度的系统，这些量在有限但远长于微观弛豫时间的时间尺度上弛豫（如动力学化学、线性粘弹性）。此前缺乏从微观动力学理论严格推导线性准流体力学的系统框架，作者希望填补这一空白。

Method: 从任意线性化、因果的类动力学理论出发，并赋予其慢自由度；证明守恒量与准守恒可观测量的精确动力学在快弛豫时间尺度上可作系统展开。其零阶结果构成因果的对称双曲理论，属于相应的瞬态流体力学普适类；高阶修正可系统计算。

Result: 零阶方程为因果的对称双曲理论，确立以色列-斯图尔特型动力学为慢弛豫模式的普适描述；高阶修正系统可计算，并继承底层微观理论的普适对称性、昂萨格关系、正定性与因果性约束。

Conclusion: 该工作为线性准流体力学提供了严格的等效场论基础，将以色列-斯图尔特型框架确立为慢弛豫模式的普适有效描述，并保证高阶修正满足物理约束。

Abstract: Quasi-hydrodynamics describes systems with quasi-conserved degrees of freedom, namely observables that relax on timescales that are finite but parametrically longer than microscopic relaxation times. Examples include kinetic chemistry and linear viscoelasticity. Here, we develop a rigorous effective-field-theory framework for linear quasi-hydrodynamics from kinetic-type theories. Starting from any linearized, causal kinetic-like theory endowed with slow degrees of freedom, we show that the exact dynamics of conserved and quasi-conserved observables admits a systematic expansion in the fast relaxation timescale. At zeroth order, the resulting equations form a causal, symmetric-hyperbolic theory belonging to the appropriate transient-hydrodynamic universality class, establishing Israel-Stewart-like dynamics as the universal description of slow relaxation modes. Higher-order corrections can be computed systematically and inherit universal symmetry, Onsager, positivity, and causality constraints from the underlying microscopic theory.
