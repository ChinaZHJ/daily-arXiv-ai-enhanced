### [1] [Deuterium Production in an Effective Field Theory Constructed from On-Shell Amplitudes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23699)
*Tim M. P. Tait*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文用有效场论（核态自由度）计算 n+p→d+γ 的热核反应率，通过贝叶斯拟合热俘获与 SLEGS 光解数据确定常数，将反应率精度提高到 0.22–0.24%（含系统误差），截断误差单独界定为 0.12%，总理论不确定度 0.25–0.27%；传播至 BBN 网络后，使原初氘丰度预测变化 -0.06%，并将该反应对 D/H 不确定度的贡献从 0.089% 降至 0.050%。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 氘的生成反应 n+p→d+γ 是原初氘丰度（D/H）预测的关键输入，其反应率不确定度是 D/H 误差预算的主要来源之一；作者希望用基于核态自由度的有效场论系统、可定量地计算该反应率并将其理论误差降至可忽略水平。

Method: 采用以核态本身为自由度的有效场论：由在壳三点顶点拼装振幅，沿线因子化道胶合，并补上符合对称性的接触项。氘核通过 d-n-p 顶点进入，归一化到实测的渐近归一化系数；核子对重散射用色散方法重求和，保留两个短程常数——等矢量磁接触相互作用和电偶极接触相互作用。对热俘获测量与 SLEGS 光解数据进行联合贝叶斯拟合，并系统涵盖对 SLEGS 数据及 P 波重散射的合理处理方式；通过单独界定展开截断误差（下一阶接触项与两个拟合常数简并，贡献 0.03%）得到总理论不确定度。

Result: 两个短程常数均为自然大小；热核反应率在大爆炸核合成窗口内确定到 0.22–0.24%，截断误差单独为 0.12%（其中下一阶接触项 0.03%），总理论不确定度为 0.25–0.27%。经 BBN 网络传播，该反应率使预测的原初氘丰度移动 -0.06%，并把该反应对 D/H 不确定度的贡献从 0.089% 削减到 0.050%。

Conclusion: 该有效场论框架将 n+p→d+γ 反应的理论误差量化到约 0.26%，并将此反应对原初氘丰度误差的贡献降至 0.050%，实际上使该反应从原初 D/H 误差预算中退隐，为 BBN 对原初氘的精确检验清除了这一不确定度来源。

Abstract: We compute the deuterium-production reaction $n+p \to d+γ$ in an effective field theory whose degrees of freedom are the nuclear states themselves: the amplitude is assembled from on-shell three-point vertices, glued across its factorization channels, and completed by the contact terms consistent with the symmetries. The deuteron enters through the $d$-$n$-$p$ vertex, normalized to the measured asymptotic normalization coefficient. Rescattering of the nucleon pair is resummed dispersively, leaving two short-distance constants, an isovector magnetic and an electric dipole contact interaction. A joint Bayesian fit to the thermal capture measurements and the SLEGS photodisintegration data finds both of natural size and determines the thermonuclear rate to 0.22-0.24% across the nucleosynthesis window, including systematics spanning the defensible treatments of the SLEGS data and of the $P$-wave rescattering. Truncating the expansion is bounded separately at 0.12%, of which the next order of contact terms -- degenerate with the two fitted constants -- supplies 0.03%, for a total theory uncertainty of 0.25-0.27%. Propagated through a BBN network, the rate shifts the predicted primordial deuterium by -0.06% and cuts this reaction's contribution to the D/H uncertainty from 0.089% to 0.050%, retiring it from the primordial D/H error budget for practical purposes.

### [2] [Deformed self-consistent Green's function method for atomic nuclei at second and third order in the algebraic diagrammatic construction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23700)
*A. Scalesi,T. Duguet,V. Somà*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文扩展了自洽格林函数（SCGF）方法，通过允许单体传播子自发破缺SU(2)旋转对称性，将其推广到双开壳（形变）原子核，实现了形变SCGF（dSCGF）方法，并以Si-28为例验证了其有效性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 第一性原理核理论的目标是从头描述原子核。多项式标度的展开方法已将亚百分精度的从头计算扩展到中等质量核和少数闭壳重核，但形变的双开壳重核和超重核仍无法处理。为填补这一空白，需要将SCGF方法推广到允许对称性自发破缺的双开壳核。

Method: 在SCGF形式体系中允许单体传播子自发破缺SU(2)旋转对称性，构建形变SCGF（dSCGF）方法；基于一阶、二阶、三阶的代数图解构造（ADC）截断，并实现在新开发的多体软件包FoxTrot中。详细讨论了处理对称性不受限的M方案工作基所需的数值策略。以1.8/2.0 (EM)哈密顿量研究Si-28，考察三核子相互作用及其秩约化近似对形变Hartree-Fock总能曲线的影响，并进行约束dSCGF计算与无约束自洽计算。

Result: 约束dSCGF得到的关联曲线与平均场曲线明显不同；关联曲线上作为极小值的物理解可从形变Hartree-Fock曲线上任意点通过无约束计算达到，证明方法具有自洽性。Si-28基态结合能（三阶，外推至无穷大基尺寸极限）与实验符合在2.1%以内，激发的长椭球解与观测到的0^+_3形状同核异能态一致。

Conclusion: 这些进展为在不远的将来对所有（超）重核进行精确的从头描述开辟了道路，使形变双开壳重核与超重核的亚百分精度从头计算成为可能。

Abstract: The description of atomic nuclei from first principles constitutes one of the central goals of nuclear theory. Polynomial-scaling expansion methods have extended \textit{ab initio} calculations at sub-percent accuracy to medium-mass nuclei and a few closed-shell heavy nuclei, but deformed doubly open-shell heavy and superheavy nuclei remain out of reach. The self-consistent Green's function (SCGF) formalism is here extended to doubly open-shell nuclei by allowing the one-body propagator to spontaneously break SU(2) rotational symmetry. The resulting deformed SCGF (dSCGF) scheme, based on the algebraic diagrammatic construction truncated at first, second, and third order, is implemented in a newly developed many-body suite, \texttt{FoxTrot}. Numerical strategies required to handle the associated, symmetry-unrestricted $M$-scheme working basis are discussed in detail. The method is illustrated through a study of $^{28}$Si based on the 1.8/2.0 (EM) Hamiltonian. The impact of the three-nucleon interaction and of its rank-reduction approximation on the deformed Hartree-Fock total energy curve is examined, and the correlated curve obtained from constrained dSCGF calculations is shown to differ appreciably from the mean-field one. Physical solutions appearing as minima of the correlated curve are shown to be reachable via unconstrained calculations starting from any point along the deformed Hartree-Fock curve, demonstrating the self-consistent character of the method. The $^{28}$Si ground-state binding energy at third order, extrapolated to the infinite basis-size limit, reproduces experiment within $2.1\%$, while the excited prolate solution is consistent with the observed $0^+_3$ shape isomer. The present developments open the way to an accurate ab initio description of all (very) heavy nuclei in the near future.

### [3] [Self-Consistent Determination of the Transition Temperature Between the $^{14}\mathrm{C}(n,γ)^{15}\mathrm{C}$ and $^{14}\mathrm{C}(p,γ)^{15}\mathrm{N}$ Reactions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24318)
*R. Ya. Kezerashvili,N. A. Burkova,A. S. Tkachenko,S. B. Dubovichenko*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 该论文首次在修正势簇模型（MPCM）框架下自洽地研究并比较了 ^14C(n,γ)^15C 与 ^14C(p,γ)^15N 两个竞争反应，确定了质子俘获超越中子俘获的转变温度，并讨论了 Tsallis 统计下的偏移。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: ^14C 的中子俘获与质子俘获反应在天体核合成中相互竞争，但此前缺乏在同一理论框架下的自洽比较；以往对转变温度的估计因使用不同模型而存在不确定性，需要改进的核物理输入。

Method: 采用修正势簇模型（MPCM），利用散射与束缚态数据约束相互作用势，计算 ^14C(p,γ0)^15N 反应的总截面、天体物理 S 因子与反应率，并结合作者此前对 ^14C(n,γ)^15C 的 MPCM 计算进行自洽比较；分别在 Maxwell–Boltzmann 统计与 Tsallis 统计下确定转变温度。

Result: 得到 ^14C(p,γ0)^15N 的天体物理 S 因子 S(0)=4.5(1) keV·b；在 Maxwell–Boltzmann 统计下预测转变温度 T9=2.5，显著高于此前估计；Tsallis 统计下偏离热平衡会使转变温度发生显著移动。

Conclusion: 该自洽计算提供了改进的核物理输入，可用于天体核合成计算，并表明非广延统计效应在确定竞争反应转变温度时不可忽略。

Abstract: We present the first self-consistent theoretical study of the competing $^{14}\mathrm{C}(n,γ)^{15}\mathrm{C}$ and $^{14}\mathrm{C}(p,γ)^{15}\mathrm{N}$ reactions within the same modified potential cluster model (MPCM). For the $^{14}$C$(p,γ_{0})^{15}$N reaction, total cross sections, astrophysical $S$ factors, and reaction rates are calculated using interaction potentials constrained by the available scattering and bound-state data. The astrophysical $S$-factor is estimated as $S(0)=4.5(1)$~keV$\cdot \text{b}$. Combining these results with our recent MPCM calculations for $^{14}\mathrm{C}(n,γ)^{15}\mathrm{C}$, we determine the transition temperature at which proton capture overtakes neutron capture in the production of $^{15}\mathrm{N}$. The self-consistent comparison predicts a transition temperature $T_9^{\rm c.p.}=2.5$ under Maxwell--Boltzmann statistics, significantly higher than previous estimates. The analysis is extended to Tsallis statistics, demonstrating that deviations from thermal equilibrium produce substantial shifts of the transition temperature. These results provide improved nuclear-physics input for astrophysical nucleosynthesis calculations.

### [4] [Resolving the $φ$-meson directed-flow puzzle by multi-step meson--baryon dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24573)
*Yingjie Zhou,Taesoo Song,Susanne Glässel,Jiaxing Zhao,Christoph Blume,Iouri Vassiliev,Vadim Voronyuk,Yaping Wang,Nu Xu,Jörg Aichelin,Elena Bratkovskaya*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 该论文利用PHQMD模型结合基于扩展SU(6)手征有效拉格朗日量的耦合道T矩阵计算，解释了STAR实验中φ介子意外大的定向流：高重子密度区中φ主要通过介子-重子/介子-超子多步反应产生，并受重子驱动及谱函数展宽影响，从而印上重子源的集体运动。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: STAR在固定靶束流能量扫描中发现Au+Au碰撞中φ介子的定向流异常大，可与质子和Λ重子相比，远强于轻奇异介子。由于φ是隐奇异介子，与非奇异强子相互作用较弱，该现象被解读为非常规重子动力学或耦合φ道的奇异重子共振信号，亟需理论解释。

Method: 作者采用Parton-Hadron-Quantum-Molecular-Dynamics（PHQMD）模型，论证高重子密度区φ介子主要经多步介子-重子及介子-超子反应产生；其跃迁振幅由基于扩展SU(6)手征有效拉格朗日量的耦合道T矩阵计算约束；并引入φ谱函数的介质展宽效应。

Result: 重子驱动的产生道与φ谱函数介质展宽共同增强了近阈φ产生，并使产生的φ介子印上富重子源的集体运动，从而在模型中重现了与质子和Λ重子相当的大的φ定向流。

Conclusion: STAR观测到的φ介子大定向流无需引入奇异重子共振等非常规机制，可由高重子密度区的重子驱动产生道及介质效应对φ谱函数的展宽加以解释，揭示了重子源集体运动向φ介子的传递机制。

Abstract: Recent STAR measurements at fixed-target Beam Energy Scan energies have revealed an unexpectedly large directed flow of $φ$ mesons in Au+Au collisions, comparable to that of protons and $Λ$ baryons and much stronger than that of light strange mesons. Since the $φ$ is a hidden-strangeness meson with relatively weak interactions with non-strange hadrons, this observation has been interpreted as a possible signal of unconventional baryonic dynamics or exotic baryonic resonances coupled to the $φ$ channel. Within the framework of the Parton-Hadron-Quantum-Molecular-Dynamics(PHQMD) model, we demonstrate that in the high baryon density region, $φ$ mesons are produced predominantly through multi-step meson--baryon and meson--hyperon reactions, whose transition amplitudes are constrained by a coupled-channel $T$-matrix calculation based on an extended SU(6) chiral effective Lagrangian. Together with the in-medium broadening of the $φ$ spectral function, these baryon-driven production channels enhance near-threshold $φ$ production and imprint the collective motion of the baryon-rich source on the produced $φ$ mesons.

### [5] [Cluster emission and its impact on the r-process nucleosynthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24499)
*Natalia Thomas,Silvia Bara,Thomas Elias Cocolios,Stephane Goriely,Boris Andel,Andrei N. Andreyev,Alberto Camaiani,James G. Cubiss,Hilde De Witte,Zoe Favier,Michael Heines,Fedor Ivandikov,Jake D. <details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 团簇发射是一种介于α衰变与裂变之间的奇特衰变模式，目前对其性质了解有限；同时，团簇发射是否能作为中子星并合中r过程核合成的衰变通道并影响元素丰度分布，尚不明确。因此需要从实验上确认团簇发射事件并定量评估其在天体核合成中的作用。

Method: 在ISOLDE（CERN）分析A=230束流谱中探测到的五个高能事件，假设其由团簇发射引起，确定最可能的母核-团簇对为230Ra发射22O，并得到分支比(4.3 ± 1.9)×10⁻⁹。随后采用四种解析公式估计一组中子丰核的团簇发射部分半衰期，为每个母核选取一组团簇核计算衰变率，并将所有团簇发射道次的速率纳入中子星并合r过程核合成计算，与未考虑团簇发射的情况对比同位素丰度分布。

Conclusion: 团簇发射作为稀有衰变模式虽在实验中得以确认（230Ra→22O），但由于现有解析公式所预测的团簇发射衰变速率过低，将其纳入中子星并合r过程核合成计算后并不会改变最终的同位素丰度分布，表明团簇发射在天体r过程核合成中的作用可以忽略。

The rates of all decay channels of cluster emission per parent nucleus were then included in calculations of the r-process nucleosynthesis in a neutron star merger in order to study the possible impact of cluster emission on the r-process nuclear production. The resulting isotopic abundance distributions were compared to those calculated for a case in which cluster emission was not considered. It was found that the inclusion of cluster emission decay rates from the simple analytical formulas available nowadays does not influence the results of the r-process nucleosynthesis.
