*Kosuke Nomura*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文利用相互作用玻色子模型（IBM），结合微观核密度泛函理论得到的哈密顿量参数，分析了Ra和Th同位素中八极关联对低能激发0^+态及双中子转移强度的影响。研究表明八极自由度能合理描述基态带能级，并显著降低0^+_2态能量，且八极关联对(p,t)和(t,p)转移强度有重要贡献。

Method: 采用相互作用玻色子模型（IBM），并引入八极自由度（octupole boson）。哈密顿量参数通过微观核密度泛函理论（nuclear density functional theory）计算得到。分析了基态带能级、激发0^+态的形成（多数由多个八极玻色子组态构成），以及0^+_1 → 0^+_2 的 (p,t) 和 (t,p) 双中子转移强度随同位素链的变化。

Abstract: Impacts of octupole correlations on low-energy excited $0^+$ states and two-neutron transfer intensities in Ra and Th isotopes are analyzed within the interacting boson model, using the Hamiltonian parameters that are obtained from microscopic nuclear density functional theory calculations. Incorporating the octupole degree of freedom in the model is shown to give an overall reasonable description of the ground-state-band energy levels and to lower the $0^+_2$ ones significantly. The calculation yields a number of excited $0^+$ states, most of which are formed by the configurations of more than one octupole boson. Octupole correlations make significant contributions to the $0^+_1 \to 0^+_2$ $(p,t)$ and $(t,p)$ transfer intensities in those nuclei in which the octupole collectivity is most enhanced. Behaviors of the predicted transfer intensities with the increasing neutron number along the isotopic chain reflect nuclear structural evolution.

### [2] [Final-state centrality in relativistic heavy-ion simulations from neural networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13828)

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 相对论重离子碰撞实验中通过末态量（通常是带电粒子多重数）确定中心度，但混合流体动力学模型模拟数百万事件成本过高，因此通常用初始熵或能量近似中心度。作者希望用神经网络以低成本复现末态中心度定义，并量化初始态与末态中心度定义的差异。

Method: 在几百个混合事件上训练神经网络，预测逐事件的带电粒子多重数和平均横动量[p_T]，相对误差仅1–2%，从而以远低于完整模拟的成本生成数百万次碰撞。然后对比初始态与末态中心度定义，并计算[p_T]的涨落及其与椭圆流代理量（由初始偏心率估计）和多重数的皮尔逊关联。

Result: 对Pb–Pb 2.76 TeV和Au–Au 200 GeV碰撞，10%宽中心度类中初始态与末态定义几乎选出相同事件，但在1%宽的类中最多有20%的事件会改变类别。[p_T]涨落与椭圆流代理量的关联对中心度定义不敏感，而与多重数的关联则显著变化。

Conclusion: 该方法可在贝叶斯分析的每个设计点上以适中的额外成本应用，实现高精度的末态中心度分类与逐事件涨落关联研究，而无需承担完整混合模拟的巨大开销。

Abstract: Experiments in relativistic heavy-ion collisions determine centrality from final-state quantities, usually the charged-particle multiplicity. Performing the same in hydrodynamic simulations requires millions of events, which is impractical for state-of-the-art hybrid models, and centrality is therefore commonly approximated by the initial entropy or energy. We show that neural networks trained on a few hundred hybrid events predict the event-by-event multiplicity and mean transverse momentum $[p_T]$ with relative errors of 1--2\%, which makes it possible to generate millions of collisions at a small fraction of the cost of the full simulation. We then compute the fluctuations of $[p_T]$ and its Pearson correlations with a proxy of the elliptic flow, estimated from the initial eccentricity, and with multiplicity. The first correlation is insensitive to how centrality is defined, while the second changes substantially. The approach can be applied at every design point of Bayesian analyses at modest additional cost.
