### [1] [Role of the $δ$ Meson in Softening the Symmetry Energy within the DDRHF Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13902)
*Qirui Li,Jinniu Hu,Ying Zhang,Hong Shen*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文在密度依赖相对论 Hartree-Fock（DDRHF）框架下，研究了同位旋矢量-标量 δ 介子对对称能密度依赖性的影响，发现引入恒定 δ 耦合可有效软化对称能（将斜率参数 L 从约 73 MeV 降至 32 MeV），同时保持饱和性质基本不变，且使中子星质量-半径关系向更小半径偏移。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 对称能及其斜率参数 L 对核物质与中子星性质至关重要，但 DDRHF 框架下仅含 σ、ω、ρ、π 介子的参数化往往给出偏大的 L 值（65–110 MeV），难以满足相关实验与天体物理约束，因此需要寻找有效的额外自由度来调控同位旋矢量性质。

Method: 作者以包含 σ、ω、ρ、π 介子且满足饱和性质经验约束的 1006 组 DDRHF 参数化为基线，从中随机选取 RHF-NK1 和 RHF-NK2 两组代表参数；随后引入 δ 介子，在相同饱和性质约束下重新优化介子-核子耦合，并考察恒定 δ 耦合的情形，最后进行逐道分解分析对称能软化机制以及计算中子星质量-半径关系。

Result: 基线参数化给出的对称能斜率参数集中在 L≈65–110 MeV；引入恒定 δ 耦合后，L 从约 73 MeV 降至 32 MeV，而每核子结合能、饱和密度、对称能及不可压缩系数几乎不变。逐道分解表明，软化并非仅来自 δ 介子的直接贡献，而是由 δ、ρ、π 介子与同位旋标量 Fock 贡献之间的重新分配共同造成；所得中子星质量-半径关系向更小半径偏移。

Conclusion: δ 介子为 DDRHF 模型提供了一个高效的额外自由度，可在保持核物质饱和性质的同时有效软化对称能、降低斜率参数 L，并用于控制模型同位旋矢量性质，进而影响中子星结构。

Abstract: We investigate the effects of the isovector-scalar $δ$ meson on the density dependence of the symmetry energy within the density-dependent relativistic Hartree--Fock (DDRHF) framework. As a baseline, we generate $1006$ accepted DDRHF parametrizations including the $σ$, $ω$, $ρ$, and $π$ mesons by imposing empirical constraints on the saturation properties of nuclear matter. The resulting symmetry-energy slope parameters are confined to relatively large values, $L\simeq65$--$110~\mathrm{MeV}$. Two representative parametrizations, denoted RHF-NK1 and RHF-NK2, are randomly selected from this ensemble. Starting from these two parametrizations, we introduce the $δ$ meson and readjust the meson--nucleon couplings under the same saturation-property constraints. The numerical optimization shows that small values of $L$ are obtained most efficiently when the $δ$ coupling is taken to be constant. In this case, $L$ is reduced from approximately $73$ to $32~\mathrm{MeV}$, while the binding energy per nucleon, saturation density, symmetry energy, and incompressibility coefficient remain nearly unchanged. A channel-by-channel decomposition shows that the softening is not caused by the direct $δ$-meson contribution alone, but by a redistribution among the $δ$, $ρ$, and $π$ mesons together with the isoscalar Fock contributions. The resulting neutron-star mass--radius relations shift toward smaller radii, indicating that the $δ$ meson provides an efficient additional degree of freedom for controlling the isovector properties of DDRHF models.

### [2] [Bayesian inference of event-by-event collision geometry from charged-particle multiplicity in heavy-ion collisions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14045)
*Yige Huang,Fu-Peng Li,Hanwen Feng,Nu Xu*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文提出一种基于贝叶斯推理的逐事件参与核子数（N_part）与二体碰撞数（N_coll）后验分布推断方法（IPD），以概率化分配替代传统的硬切中心度分类，并验证其能改善净质子累积量的重建。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统中心度分类依赖于硬切的多重数区间，会因多重数-几何关联的模糊性（smearing）和体积涨落引入偏差，影响下游观测量的准确性。

Method: IPD 框架以 Monte-Carlo Glauber 模型得到的 (N_part, N_coll) 联合分布为先验，以根据带电粒子多重数涨落标定的负二项分布为似然，通过贝叶斯推理得到逐事件的 N_part 与 N_coll 后验分布。

Result: 在 19.6 GeV 的 UrQMD-MCG 混合模型上进行的闭箱测试表明，IPD 方法得到的后验分布标定良好、偏差可忽略，且相比传统基于多重数的中心度选择，显著改善了净质子累积量的重建。

Conclusion: IPD 方法通过概率化处理多重数-几何关联，将模糊性显式化，降低了体积涨落对下游观测量的影响，是一种更稳健的中心度选择替代方案。

Abstract: We propose the Inference-driven Participant Determination (IPD) method, a Bayesian framework for inferring event-by-event posterior distributions of the number of participants ($N_{\text{part}}$) and binary collisions ($N_{\text{coll}}$) from final-state charged-particle multiplicities in relativistic heavy-ion collisions. The joint distribution of $(N_{\text{part}}, N_{\text{coll}})$ obtained from the Monte-Carlo Glauber model is used as the prior, while negative binomial distributions calibrated to charged-particle multiplicity fluctuations define the likelihood. This approach replaces conventional hard-cut centrality classification with a probabilistic assignment based on $N_{\text{part}}$, making the multiplicity--geometry smearing explicit and reducing the impact of volume fluctuations on downstream observables. A closure test using an UrQMD-MCG hybrid model at $\sqrt{s_{NN}} = 19.6$~GeV shows that the method yields well-calibrated posterior distributions with negligible bias and improves the reconstruction of net-proton cumulants relative to conventional multiplicity-based centrality selection.

### [3] [Neural network maximum entropy framework for distribution reconstruction in heavy-ion collisions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14056)
*Qian-Ru Lin,Fu-Peng Li,YiGe Huang,Long-Gang Pang*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文提出一种神经网络+最大熵（NN+MaxEnt）框架，用于从重离子碰撞中的有限观测量重建概率分布，并在闭合检验和真实物理问题（喷注能量损失分布）上验证了其有效性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 从有限观测量重建底层概率分布是重离子碰撞中的核心逆问题。传统方法往往依赖固定的解析形式或严格的先验假设，缺乏灵活性；而纯神经网络方法又可能过拟合、不满足正性与归一化约束。因此需要一种兼顾表达灵活性与物理约束（正性、归一化）的正则化重建框架，减少对特定拟设（ansatz）的依赖。

Method: 提出 NN+MaxEnt 框架：用灵活的神经网络表示概率分布，并以 Shannon 熵作为正则项，同时通过构造保证分布的正性与归一化，无需预设解析函数形式。观测量通过可微的正向映射（forward map）与分布关联，从而可用梯度法联合优化。首先用高斯、泊松、混合泊松等闭合检验验证方法；随后应用于两个物理逆问题：（1）由泛函重整化群（fRG）累积量约束的有效多重数重建（作为闭合检验）；（2）由 Pb+Pb 碰撞 $\sqrt{s_{NN}}=2.76$ TeV 单举喷注 $R_{AA}$ 数据提取条件喷注能量损失分布。

Result: 在 fRG 闭合检验中，NN+MaxEnt 精确复现了所施加的累积量，所得分布与传统 MaxEnt 解一致。在喷注问题中，重建的能量损失分布能复现实测 $R_{AA}$；当初态喷注动量 $x=50$ GeV 时，条件平均能量损失为 $\langle Δp_T\rangle \simeq 11.8$ GeV，中心 16–84% 区间为 9.0–15.0 GeV。提取的能量损失轮廓与贝叶斯 MCMC 及 LBT 结果定性一致。

Conclusion: NN+MaxEnt 为通过可微正向映射从观测量正则化重建分布提供了一种灵活、对拟设依赖较小的方法，兼具神经网络表征能力与最大熵正则化，并在闭合检验与真实喷注能量损失重建中表现良好。

Abstract: We develop a neural-network maximum-entropy (NN+MaxEnt) framework for reconstructing probability distributions from limited observables in heavy-ion collisions. The method combines flexible neural-network representations with Shannon-entropy regularization, preserving positivity and normalization without assuming a fixed analytic form. After validation with Gaussian, Poisson, and mixed-Poisson closure tests, we apply the framework to two physics-motivated inverse problems: an effective multiplicity reconstruction constrained by functional renormalization group cumulants, used as a closure test, and the conditional jet-energy-loss distribution extracted from single-inclusive jet $R_{AA}$ data in Pb+Pb collisions at $\sqrt{s_{NN}}=2.76$~TeV. For the fRG closure test, NN+MaxEnt accurately reproduces the imposed cumulants and yields distributions consistent with conventional MaxEnt solutions. For jets, the reconstructed energy-loss distributions reproduce the measured $R_{AA}$; at an initial jet momentum $x=50~\mathrm{GeV}$, the conditional mean energy loss is $\langleΔp_T\rangle\simeq11.8~\mathrm{GeV}$, with a central $16\text{--}84\%$ interval of $9.0\text{--}15.0~\mathrm{GeV}$. The extracted energy-loss profile is qualitatively consistent with Bayesian MCMC and LBT results. NN+MaxEnt thus provides a flexible, less ansatz-dependent framework for regularized distribution reconstruction from observables connected to the underlying distribution through differentiable forward maps.

### [4] [Enhancement of alpha-decay by positive hexadecapole deformation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14202)
*Kai Ren,Minghui Hu,Pengfei Ma,Junlong Tian,Cheng Li*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文揭示α衰变中十六极形变β4影响的争议源于壳效应处理方式：未显式修正壳效应时，幻数附近的残差被吸收进形变系数，掩盖了真实的β4依赖。通过在Royer公式中引入逆Casten因子C_pn和母核β4形变，RMS偏差从0.309降至0.184，证明正的十六极形变系统地缩短半衰期。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 关于十六极形变β4是否影响α衰变存在争议：机器学习分析显示其与团簇预形成强相关，而经验公式仅发现微弱效应。作者旨在阐明该分歧的根源，并给出真实β4依赖的定量证据。

Method: 在Royer公式中加入显式壳修正——逆Casten因子C_pn（编码价质子-中子相对最近闭壳的关联），以及母核形变β4^(p)，对192个偶偶核进行拟合；并将相同修正应用于UDL公式，外推预测1060个偶偶核。

Result: 加入两项修正后RMS偏差从0.309降至0.184；拟合的β4^(p)系数为负，表明正的十六极形变系统地缩短半衰期，与局部凸面区域增强的α团簇预形成一致。在Z=94的Pu同位素链（β4^(p)>0显著），原Royer公式高估半衰期约0.5 dex，构成可检验的表面预形成效应信号。

Conclusion: 十六极形变确实影响α衰变，但需先显式扣除壳效应才能揭示；正的β4通过局部凸面增强α团簇预形成而缩短半衰期。该修正同时改善Royer和UDL公式，并为大量偶偶核提供预测。

Abstract: Whether hexadecapole deformation ($β_4$) influences $α$ decay remains controversial: machine-learning analyses suggest a strong link to cluster preformation, while empirical formulas find only marginal effects. We show that this discrepancy originates in the treatment of shell effects---without an explicit shell correction, residuals near magic numbers are absorbed into the deformation coefficients, obscuring the genuine $β_4$ dependence. Adding the inverse Casten factor $C_{pn}$, which encodes valence proton--neutron correlations relative to the nearest closed shells, and the parent-nucleus deformation $β_4^{(p)}$ to the Royer formula reduces the root-mean-square deviation from 0.309 to 0.184 for 192 even--even nuclei. The fitted negative $β_4^{(p)}$ coefficient shows that positive hexadecapole deformation systematically shortens half-lives, consistent with enhanced $α$-cluster preformation at locally convex surface regions. For the $Z=94$ isotopic chain (Pu), where pronounced $β_4^{(p)}>0$ occurs, the original Royer formula overestimates half-lives by up to $\sim\!0.5$~dex, providing a clear, testable signature of this surface-preformation effect. The same correction also improves the UDL and yields predictions for 1060 even--even nuclei.

### [6] [Extraction of Neutron-Skin Parameters in Zr+Zr and Ru+Ru Collisions at $\sqrt{s_{\text{NN}}}$ = 200 GeV](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13935)
*Junjian Zhou,Dongsheng Li,Fan Si,Xiujun Li,Yifei Zhang*

Main category: nucl-ex

TL;DR: 该论文研究了同位旋相关的核子密度分布（中子皮效应）对高能重离子碰撞中重子输运观测量的影响，通过对比96Ru+96Ru与96Zr+96Zr碰撞，提取了两同量异位素体系间相对中子皮厚度差为0.278 ± 0.018 fm，并指出核结构效应在无重子结假设下即可解释R_{B/Q}>1现象。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 重离子碰撞中初始核几何（质子与中子的密度分布）显著影响末态效应，而中子皮效应可能对重子输运观测量产生不可忽略的影响。此前R_{B/Q}>1现象常被归因于重子结（baryon junction）假设，作者希望考察核结构效应是否能在不引入该假设的情况下解释这一现象。

Method: 在UrQMD模型中引入同位旋相关的Woods-Saxon分布以描述中子皮效应，研究√s_NN=200 GeV的96Ru+96Ru与96Zr+96Zr碰撞；系统扫描96Zr核的质子表面弥散度参数a_{p,Zr}，通过与STAR实验数据的χ²分析，利用净电荷产额差ΔQ提取相对中子皮厚度差。

Abstract: The initial nuclear geometry of colliding nuclei, manifested in proton and neutron density profiles, plays a key role in determining final-state effects in high-energy heavy-ion collisions. We study ${^{96}\text{Ru}}+{^{96}\text{Ru}}$ and ${^{96}\text{Zr}}+{^{96}\text{Zr}}$ collisions at $\sqrt{s_{\text{NN}}}=200$ GeV incorporating an isospin-dependent Woods-Saxon distribution to account for neutron-skin effects in the UrQMD model. We further show that the neutron-skin effect evidently modifies the baryon-to-charge transport ratio $R_{B/Q}=\langle B \rangle/ΔQ \times ΔZ/A$, and substantially influences the observed $R_{B/Q}>1$ behavior in central collisions. This work indicates that nuclear structure effects contribute non-negligibly to baryon transport observables without a baryon junction hypothesis, and underscores the importance of precise initial-state geometry constraints for exploring novel QCD phenomena in relativistic heavy-ion collisions.
