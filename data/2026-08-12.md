### [1] [Nucleon electromagnetic form-factors in a minimal Gari-Krümpelmann model including the explicit two-pion continuum](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10907)
*Wulf Krümpelmann,Yong-Hui Lin,Ulf-G. Meißner*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文扩展了Gari–Krümpelmann模型，在等矢量通道中显式纳入色散双π连续谱贡献，用现代色散分析替代唯象ρ介子参数化，仅用5个参数即可描述空间区域电子-质子散射的大批形状因子数据和微分截面。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 原有GK模型虽成功结合矢量介子主导与pQCD渐进约束，但对ρ介子的处理较为唯象，未能清晰分离长程连续谱动力学、相干矢量介子贡献与直接夸克流动力学。作者旨在通过显式引入色散双π连续谱，实现更清晰的物理机制分离，并减少参数数量。

Method: 在GK模型的内禀高Q²结构中嵌入来自现代色散分析的双π贡献，将物理ρ动力学重新解释为关联双π谱函数的一部分；保留直接耦合项以实现向渐进区域的过渡。等矢量通道中，用色散双π连续谱替代原有唯象ρ介子参数化。

Result: 仅用5个参数即可成功描述空间类区域电子-质子散射的大量形状因子数据和微分截面，实现了长程连续谱动力学、相干矢量介子贡献与短程夸克流机制的清晰分离。

Conclusion: 显式纳入色散双π连续谱不仅保持了GK模型原有的预言能力，还使物理图像更为清晰：ρ介子不再是独立自由度，而是双π关联谱函数的表现。该框架为电磁核子形状因子的统一描述提供了更坚实的色散理论基础。

Abstract: The Gari--Krümpelmann (GK) model provided one of the first semiphenomenological descriptions of the electromagnetic nucleon form-factors over a wide range of momentum transfer. It combined vector-meson dominance at low and intermediate momentum transfer with perturbative-QCD constraints at large momentum transfer. The present work extends this framework by including the dispersive two-pion continuum explicitly in the isovector channel. The two-pion contribution is taken from modern dispersive analyses and embedded into the intrinsic high-$Q^2$ structure of the GK ansatz. The physical $ρ$ dynamics is then interpreted as part of the correlated two-pion spectral function. The direct coupling terms remain essential for the transition to the asymptotic regime. This gives a cleaner separation between long-range continuum dynamics, coherent vector-meson contributions, and direct quark-current dynamics. With just 5 parameters, we can describe the large body on form-factor data and differential cross sections from electron-proton scattering in the space-like region.

Abstract: Atomic nuclei are strongly correlated quantum many-body systems, and how their shell structure evolves with increasing neutron excess remains a central open question in nuclear physics. Calcium isotopes are an ideal testing ground: alongside the traditional magic numbers $N=20,28$, new shell closures have been proposed at $N=32,34$ ($^{52,54}\mathrm{Ca}$). While the charge radius rises rapidly towards $N=32$, further moments and radii in the isotopic chain have remained inaccessible due to the low production yield of a few ions per second. This provides strong evidence for a robust $N=32$ shell closure and stringently constrains nuclear structure models.
