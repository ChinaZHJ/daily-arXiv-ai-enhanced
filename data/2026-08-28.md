### [2] [Single-particle potentials in asymmetric nuclear matter within the LOCV framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26343)
*Zahra Ziarati,Hamidreza Moshfegh*

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 本文采用最低阶约束变分（LOCV）方法，系统研究了非对称核物质中质子与中子的单粒子势和有效质量，并考察了它们对动量、密度及同位旋非对称度的依赖关系，同时分析三体力效应，并与其他理论及经验约束的Lane势进行了对比。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 精确确定非对称核物质中核子的单粒子势与有效质量对于理解同位旋非对称核物质性质及核结构、中子星物理等至关重要，但此类量对动量、密度和同位旋非对称度的微观依赖尚需系统研究。

Method: 采用最低阶约束变分（LOCV）方法，基于 Reid68 和 AV18 核子-核子相互作用，并纳入三体力效应，计算质子与中子的单粒子势和有效质量，将其按不同相互作用道分解，分析其对动量、密度和同位旋非对称度的依赖。

Result: 中子和质子的单粒子势随非对称度增加呈现系统性劈裂；其按相互作用道分解可识别主导的介质内相互作用贡献。对称势随动量增大而减小，且在低动量区对密度和三体力表现出显著依赖。中子-质子有效质量劈裂随非对称度增大，且中子有效质量大于质子。计算所得的对称势与其他微观、唯象方法及经验约束的Lane势一致，均随动能增加而呈下降趋势。

Conclusion: 该研究提供了一个微观的、状态依赖的非对称核物质单粒子势，其动量、密度与非对称度依赖由两体和三体相互作用直接决定，为理解和约束非对称核物质性质提供了可靠的微观基础。

Abstract: The single-particle potentials and effective masses of protons and neutrons in asymmetric nuclear matter are investigated within the lowest-order constrained variational (LOCV) method. The dependence of these quantities on momentum, density, and isospin asymmetry is studied using the $Reid68$ and $AV_{18}$ nucleon-nucleon interactions, with the effects of three-body forces also examined. The neutron and proton single-particle potentials exhibit a systematic splitting with increasing asymmetry, while their decomposition into different interaction channels identifies the dominant contributions to the in-medium interaction. The symmetry potential decreases with increasing momentum and shows a pronounced dependence on density and three-body forces, particularly at low momentum. The neutron-proton effective-mass splitting increases with asymmetry, with the neutron effective mass larger than the proton effective mass. The calculated symmetry potential is also compared with results from other microscopic and phenomenological approaches and with the empirically constrained Lane potential, showing a consistent decreasing trend with increasing kinetic energy. These results provide a microscopic, state-dependent single-particle potential for asymmetric nuclear matter, with its momentum, density, and asymmetry dependence determined directly from the underlying two and three-body interactions.

### [3] [From twelve to three active qubits: Ancilla-recycled rodeo filtering for trapped neutron-proton scattering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26569)

Main category: nucl-th

TL;DR: 论文提出一种"ancilla 回收"的动态 rodeo 滤波方案,通过在 IBM 量子硬件上用中间测量与复位复用单一辅助比特,将量子线路宽度从 ns+R 压缩至 ns+1,并在中子-质子散射的有限约束模型中验证了与静态方案一致的滤波精度与物理结果。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 静态 rodeo 滤波将所有测量推迟,需要 ns+R 个活跃量子比特,其中 R 为能量询问次数。当系统增大或询问次数 R 增加时,量子比特宽度成为瓶颈。作者希望利用中间测量与复位技术复用单一辅助比特,在不改变理想滤波器的前提下压缩线路宽度,为核物理寄存器腾出更多量子比特,同时保持有限约束散射输入。

Method: 采用 ancilla 回收策略:对 R 次辅助能量询问,在每次询问后进行中间测量并复位该辅助比特,从而将宽度降为 ns+1。具体验证中,以受限(陷俘)谱输入模拟中子-质子散射,IonQ Forte-1 上运行静态 R=10 电路(12 个活跃量子比特),IBM Aachen 上运行动态电路(仅 3 个量子比特,宽度减少 75%);将测得的滤波中心通过有限约束修正的有效力程展开(MERE)映射为 K3.7=p·cotδ0,3.7,并与精确值比较;同时检查四组态支撑是否完整,以基于采样的量子对角化再现 4×4 有效空间能级。

Result: 动态电路宽度从 12 降至 3(减少 75%)。IBM 受控交织扫描给出 ΔEc=-0.017±0.507 keV,与静态结果 -0.568±0.694 keV 相当。映射得到的 K3.7 在 IonQ 为 0.13419±0.00020 fm⁻¹,在 IBM 交织扫描为 0.13435±0.00015 fm⁻¹,均与精确值 0.13436 fm⁻¹ 一致。两种实现均保留完整四组态支持并再现精确 4×4 有效空间能级。但三个 IBM 批次暴露了超出有限取样涨落的运行间中心漂移,而后选择衰减相对更稳定。

Conclusion: ancilla 回收使 rodeo 宽度与 R 无关,释放了核寄存器所需量子比特并保留有限约束散射输入,代价是以中间线路延迟换取空间资源,并对运行可重复性提出更高要求。

Abstract: Rodeo filtering applies \(R\) ancilla-assisted energy interrogations. If all measurements are deferred, a static realization requires \(n_s+R\) active qubits for an \(n_s\)-qubit system, whereas mid-circuit measurement and reset allow one ancilla to be recycled and reduce the width to \(n_s+1\) without changing the ideal filter. We demonstrate this compression for a trapped-spectrum input to model neutron--proton scattering. A static \(R=10\) circuit on IonQ Forte-1 uses 12 active qubits, while a dynamic circuit on IBM Aachen uses only three, a 75\% reduction. Both implementations retain complete four-configuration support and therefore reproduce the exact \(4\times4\) effective-space level by sample-based quantum diagonalization. Three IBM batches nevertheless expose run-dependent center variations beyond finite-shot fluctuations, while the postselection attenuation is more stable. Ancilla recycling therefore makes the rodeo width independent of \(R\), freeing qubits for the nuclear register while preserving a finite-confinement scattering input, but exchanges spatial resources for mid-circuit latency and repeatability requirements.
