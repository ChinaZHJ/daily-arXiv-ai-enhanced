### [1] [Background characterization in the PIKACHU Phase 1 search for two-neutrino double-beta decay of $^{160}$Gd](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31837)
*T. Omori,T. Iida,A. Gando,N. Hinohara,K. Hosokawa,S. Ishidate,M. Ishigami,T. Kishimoto,K. Mizukoshi,T. Sako,S. Sugi,M. Yoshino,K. Fushimi,K. Kamada,Y. Shoji,Y. Suga,H. Suzuki,K. Takahashi*

Main category: nucl-ex

TL;DR: PIKACHU实验第一阶段利用Ce:Gd₃Al₂Ga₃O₁₂闪烁体在神冈地下实验室开展¹⁶⁰Gd双中微子双β衰变搜索，通过延迟符合分析与GEANT4模拟谱拟合建立背景模型，未观测到显著信号，给出90%置信水平下半衰期下限 T^{2ν}_{1/2} ≥ 2.32×10¹⁹ 年。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 为了搜索¹⁶⁰Gd的双中微子双β（2ν2β）衰变，需要对PIKACHU实验第一阶段的实验背景进行系统表征，建立可靠的背景模型以提高对稀有衰变信号的探测灵敏度。

### [2] [Softer charged-hadron spectra in Ne+Ne than in O+O collisions at the same multiplicity at $\sqrt{s_{\rm NN}} = 5.36$ TeV](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32516)
*Kishora Nayak*

Main category: nucl-ex

TL;DR: 本文比较了在同一带电粒子多重数（而非同一中心度）下 ATLAS 测得的 O+O 与 Ne+Ne 碰撞（√sNN=5.36 TeV）的带电强子谱，以检验给定多重数下横动量谱的形状是否依赖碰撞系统的大小。结果发现，在相同多重数下 Ne+Ne 的平均横动量 ⟨pT⟩ 低于 O+O，且 Ne+Ne 谱可由 O+O 谱沿横动量轴压缩一个拉伸因子来描述，说明更大横截面积的碰撞产生更软的谱。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 检验在给定带电粒子多重数时，横动量谱形状是否依赖于碰撞系统的大小，从而探究谱的软硬与碰撞几何（横截面积）之间的关系。

Method: 将 ATLAS 在 O+O 与 Ne+Ne 碰撞中测得的带电强子谱按相同带电粒子多重数（而非相同中心度）进行比较；直接使用已发表的 ⟨pT⟩ 值计算 Ne+Ne/O+O 比值，并对 0.4 < pT < 2 GeV 区间的谱采用单一拉伸因子 λNe/O 沿横动量轴压缩 O+O 谱来拟合 Ne+Ne 谱。同一方法也用于 Xe+Xe 与 Pb+Pb 碰撞的检验。

Result: 在相同多重数下，Ne+Ne 的 ⟨pT⟩ 低于 O+O；在 3-4% 中心度处，Ne+Ne/O+O 的 ⟨pT⟩ 比值为 0.9822，低于 1 达 6.6 个标准差（即使将 O+O 系统不确定度视为中心度间不相关）。谱的拉伸因子 λNe/O = 0.969，低于 1 达 9.5 或 3.3 个标准差（取决于 O+O 系统不确定度的公共部分是否在中心度间抵消）。这一排序符合 Ne+Ne 在相同多重数下横截面积更大、从而谱更软的预期。软化程度向边缘碰撞减小，且在前向赝快度区相同。在中心碰撞中谱形更贴近中心度而非多重数。

Conclusion: 在相同带电粒子多重数下，横动量谱形状确实依赖于碰撞系统大小：横截面积更大的系统（Ne+Ne 相对 O+O）产生更软的谱，表现为更低的 ⟨pT⟩ 和小于 1 的拉伸因子 λ。这种系统大小依赖随碰撞中心度和赝快度呈现规律性变化，并通过 Xe+Xe 与 Pb+Pb 碰撞得到进一步检验。

Abstract: Charged-hadron spectra measured by the ATLAS Collaboration in O+O and Ne+Ne collisions at $\sqrt{s_{\rm NN}} = 5.36$ TeV are compared at the same charged-particle multiplicity instead of at the same centrality, to test whether the shape of the transverse-momentum spectrum at a given multiplicity depends on the size of the colliding system. At equal multiplicity, Ne+Ne collisions have a lower mean transverse momentum $\langle p_{\rm T}\rangle$ than O+O collisions. Without any spectral fit, the published $\langle p_{\rm T}\rangle$ values give a Ne+Ne/O+O ratio of 0.9822 at 3-4% centrality, 6.6 standard deviations below unity even if the O+O systematic uncertainties are taken as uncorrelated between centrality classes. At $0.4 < p_{\rm T} < 2$ GeV the Ne+Ne spectrum is described by the O+O spectrum with the transverse-momentum axis compressed by a single stretch factor, $λ_{\rm Ne/O} = 0.969$ in the 3-4% class. It lies 9.5 or 3.3 standard deviations below unity, depending on whether the common part of the O+O systematic uncertainties cancels between centrality classes. This ordering is expected if Ne+Ne collisions have a larger transverse area at the same multiplicity and a larger area gives a softer spectrum. The softening decreases towards peripheral collisions and is the same at forward pseudorapidity. In central collisions the spectral shape follows the centrality class more closely than the multiplicity. The same procedure is tested in Xe+Xe and Pb+Pb collisions.

TL;DR: 本文建立了包含核超精细混合（NHM）的相对论非弹性正电子散射核激发（NEIPS）理论，并将其应用于类氢 ^{229}Th^{89+} 离子。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探索核超精细混合在核激发过程中的作用，并寻找增强非弹性正电子散射核激发效率的机制。

Method: 目标离子用修饰后的超精细态描述，入射与出射正电子连续态采用相对论扭曲波方法处理；计算了正电子能量从 10^2 到 10^5 eV 范围内超精细分辨的激发截面。

Conclusion: NHM 为增强 NEIPS 提供了一种高效机制，并拓宽了 NHM 在其中起重要作用的核激发过程的范围。

Abstract: We develop a relativistic theory of nuclear excitation by inelastic positron scattering (NEIPS) with nuclear hyperfine mixing (NHM) explicitly included and apply it to hydrogenlike $^{229}\mathrm{Th}^{89+}$ ions. The target ion is described by dressed hyperfine states, while the incident and outgoing positron continuum states are treated within a relativistic distorted-wave approach. We calculate hyperfine-resolved excitation cross sections for incident positron energies from $10^{2}$ to $10^{5}~\mathrm{eV}$. The cross sections increase strongly with the positron energy, with the $F_g=2\rightarrow F_e=2$ channel exhibiting the largest value. Comparison with NEIPS of the bare $^{229}\mathrm{Th}^{90+}$ nucleus shows that NHM enhances the excitation cross sections by about four orders of magnitude. These results demonstrate that NHM provides an efficient mechanism for enhancing NEIPS and broaden the range of nuclear excitation processes in which NHM plays a significant role.

TL;DR: 本文重新研究了相对论TDDFT+BCS框架下裂变碎片总动能（TKE）的计算，通过引入粒子数投影（PNP）并扩展集体空间（加入十六极与非轴对称形变），显著改善了240Pu和252Cf的TKE质量依赖关系与实验值的符合度。

Together, these reproduce the empirical TKE mass dependence substantially better than standard calculations, indicating the discrepancy reflects missing symmetry restoration and configuration space rather than a deficient energy functional.

### [6] [Neural Network Potential Reconstruction from Phase Shifts in Resonant $n+{}^{16}\mathrm{O}$ Scattering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32643)

TL;DR: 本文通过高斯包络神经网络参数化结合可微变相位计算，从弹性相移重建出 n+16O 窄 D_{3/2} 共振的局域、能量无关有效势，并分析了反演中的径向模糊性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 采用高斯包络神经网络对势进行参数化，将其与可微的变相位（variable-phase）计算耦合，并借助独立的径向薛定谔方程求解器进行交叉验证；同时用 n+α 的宽 P_{3/2} 共振作为同一方法的基准测试。
